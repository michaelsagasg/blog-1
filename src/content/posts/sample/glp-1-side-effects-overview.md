Stomach symptoms are the most common side effects. They usually appear when a dose goes up and ease over the following weeks.

## Most reported

1. Nausea
2. Diarrhea
3. Vomiting
4. Constipation

> Slow dose increases reduce early dropouts in trials.

## When to get advice

Ask a clinician about severe stomach pain or vomiting that stops you keeping fluids down.
