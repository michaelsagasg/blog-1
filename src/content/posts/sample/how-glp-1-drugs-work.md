GLP-1 drugs copy a hormone your gut releases after eating. It tells the brain you are full and slows how fast the stomach empties.

## What they change

- Appetite drops.
- Food leaves the stomach more slowly.
- Insulin release rises when blood sugar is high.

## How the drugs differ

| Drug | Targets | Average weight loss in trials |
|---|---|---|
| Semaglutide | GLP-1 | about 15% |
| Tirzepatide | GLP-1, GIP | about 21% |
| Retatrutide | GLP-1, GIP, glucagon | 24.2% at 48 weeks (phase 2) |

### A note on numbers

Trial lengths differ, so the comparison is rough.
