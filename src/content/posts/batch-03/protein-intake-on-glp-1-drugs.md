A common target for people losing weight is 1.2 to 1.6 g of protein per kg of body weight per day, which is 0.55 to 0.73 g per pound. On appetite-suppressing drugs, reaching that takes deliberate planning because you feel full quickly and eat less overall.

Retatrutide is investigational, but the same nutrition principles apply to semaglutide, tirzepatide, and other drugs in the class.

## Why protein matters more on these drugs

Weight loss costs some muscle. Protein, combined with resistance exercise, reduces that loss. Protein also increases fullness and has a higher thermic effect than fat or carbohydrate, meaning the body uses more energy to digest it.

When appetite drops, protein is often the first casualty, because people eat whatever is easy, such as crackers or sweets. Prioritizing protein guards against that.

## How much, in numbers

| Body weight | 1.2 g/kg | 1.6 g/kg |
|---|---|---|
| 70 kg (154 lb) | 84 g | 112 g |
| 90 kg (198 lb) | 108 g | 144 g |
| 110 kg (243 lb) | 132 g | 176 g |
| 130 kg (287 lb) | 156 g | 208 g |

People with higher body fat sometimes use goal weight or an adjusted weight rather than current weight, to avoid very large totals. A dietitian can help set a target.

## Protein per meal

Muscle protein synthesis responds best to about 25 to 40 g per meal. Spreading protein over three or four eating occasions works better than one large serving.

## Protein-dense foods

| Food | Protein (approx.) |

| Eggs, 2 large | 12 g |

| Canned tuna, 1 can | 25 g |

| Tofu, 150 g | 15 g |

## Tips for eating protein when you have no appetite

1. **Eat protein first** at each meal, then other foods if there is room.
2. **Use liquids.** Shakes, kefir, and smoothies go down easier when solid food is unappealing.
3. **Choose soft, moist options.** Eggs, fish, yogurt, and tofu are gentler than dry meat.
4. **Small frequent meals** can help if large portions cause nausea.
5. **Keep easy options** such as protein bars, boiled eggs, and cheese sticks.
6. **Cook in batches** so protein is ready when energy is low.

## Kidney and other concerns

For people with healthy kidneys, protein at this level is considered safe. People with chronic kidney disease may need to limit protein and should get advice from a nephrologist or dietitian. Very high protein without enough fiber and fluids can worsen constipation, a common issue on these drugs.

## Fiber and fluid

Pair protein with fiber from vegetables, beans, and whole grains, and drink enough. Constipation is a frequent side effect, and a high-protein, low-fiber diet makes it worse.

## Do you need protein powder?

Not necessarily, but it helps many people reach targets. Choose a product with a simple ingredient list and check third-party testing, since supplements are less regulated than drugs.

## Resistance training is the other half

Protein alone does not preserve muscle. Two to three strength sessions a week give the signal to keep it. The combination works better than either one alone.

## Availability

Retatrutide is not approved and has no official nutrition guidance. These targets come from general weight-loss and obesity medicine recommendations.

## Sample day at 110 g of protein on a small appetite

- **Breakfast:** Greek yogurt with berries and a scoop of protein powder mixed in, about 35 g.
- **Lunch:** Chicken or tofu with vegetables and a small portion of rice, about 30 g.
- **Snack:** Cottage cheese or two boiled eggs, about 12 g.
- **Dinner:** Fish or lean beef with vegetables, about 30 g.

That reaches roughly 107 g with modest portions. On days with nausea, swap in a protein shake and soup.

## Signs you may not be eating enough protein

- Losing strength in workouts
- Hair shedding, alongside rapid weight loss
- Feeling weak or dizzy
- Slow recovery from exercise
- Constant hunger for sweets despite the appetite drug

A dietitian can review a food log and adjust targets, especially for older adults and people with kidney disease.

## Bottom line

Aim for roughly 1.2 to 1.6 g of protein per kg per day, spread across meals, and pair it with strength training. Plan ahead, because appetite will not remind you.
