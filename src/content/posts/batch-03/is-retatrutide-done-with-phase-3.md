No, retatrutide is not done with phase 3. One phase 3 trial, TRIUMPH-4, has reported topline results. Others in the TRIUMPH and TRANSCEND programs are still running, and Eli Lilly has not yet submitted a complete application to regulators, as far as public reports show.

Status changes quickly, so check company announcements and ClinicalTrials.gov for updates.

## What has finished

- **TRIUMPH-4:** obesity with knee osteoarthritis. Topline reported: 28.7% average weight loss at 68 weeks on 12 mg.

## What is still ongoing

- **TRIUMPH-1:** obesity or overweight, with sleep apnea and osteoarthritis sub-studies.
- **TRIUMPH-2:** obesity with type 2 diabetes.
- **TRIUMPH-3:** obesity with cardiovascular disease.
- **TRIUMPH-OUTCOMES:** cardiovascular and kidney outcomes.
- **TRANSCEND-T2D trials:** type 2 diabetes.

These have different end dates, and outcomes trials run the longest.

## What "done" would mean

A drug is done with phase 3 when all the pivotal trials for its application have reported. For obesity drugs, regulators generally want:

1. At least one or two large, randomized, placebo-controlled trials on weight loss.
2. Enough safety exposure, often thousands of people treated for a year or more.
3. Evidence in relevant groups, such as those with diabetes.

Cardiovascular outcomes data may be required before or after approval, depending on the regulator.

## Could Lilly file before every trial finishes?

Yes. Companies often submit once the key weight-loss trials have reported, while outcomes trials continue. Semaglutide and tirzepatide reached the market that way. A submission for retatrutide would probably depend on the results of TRIUMPH-1, TRIUMPH-2, and TRIUMPH-3, alongside TRIUMPH-4.

## What comes after phase 3

1. Submit the application to regulators.
2. Regulators review it, typically 10 to 12 months for a standard review of a new molecular entity in the US, and shorter with priority review.
3. Regulators may ask questions or request more data.
4. If approved, the drug gets a label, and launch follows.

## Why you may see conflicting claims

Online, phase 2 and phase 3 are often mixed up. "Phase 3 results" sometimes refers to TRIUMPH-4 and sometimes to older phase 2 data. A trial being "in phase 3" is not the same as having finished it.

## How to check status yourself

- Search each trial on ClinicalTrials.gov and look at "status" and "primary completion date."
- Read Lilly's press releases and quarterly earnings materials.
- Check regulator announcements, such as the FDA's approvals pages.

## Availability

Retatrutide is not approved and cannot be prescribed. Trials continue to enroll or follow participants. Products sold online are not part of any approved supply.

## Comparing to other approved obesity drugs at the same stage

Tirzepatide's SURMOUNT program and semaglutide's STEP program each included several phase 3 trials that reported over about two years before the drugs were approved. Tirzepatide's first obesity trial, SURMOUNT-1, reported in mid-2022 and the FDA approved the drug for weight loss in November 2023. That gives a sense of a typical gap from key phase 3 data to approval of roughly a year and a half, though each drug differs.

## How long a phase 3 lasts

Obesity phase 3 trials typically last 68 to 80 weeks of treatment, plus follow-up. Outcomes trials last much longer because they wait for events like heart attacks. That is why a drug can be on the market while an outcomes trial is still running.

## Success rates

About 55% to 60% of drugs that enter phase 3 go on to a regulatory submission, according to industry analyses. The rate is higher for drugs with strong phase 2 data and lower in areas like neuroscience. A strong phase 3 result, like TRIUMPH-4, raises confidence but does not guarantee approval.

## What can still go wrong

- A safety signal in a larger group, such as heart rhythm or liver effects.
- Dropout rates at high doses that make the top dose impractical.
- Manufacturing or supply problems.
- Regulators asking for more data on long-term safety.

## Reading news of a filing

When Lilly announces it has submitted an application, that is the signal that the review clock has started. Companies often state the expected decision window in earnings calls. An FDA acceptance letter comes about 60 days after submission. Until then, no approval date can be estimated with confidence.

## Bottom line

Retatrutide has one phase 3 readout and several more coming. Expect more results over time, followed by a regulatory review if the data holds up.
