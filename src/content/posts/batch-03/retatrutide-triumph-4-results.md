TRIUMPH-4 tested retatrutide in adults with obesity and knee osteoarthritis. At 68 weeks, the 12 mg group lost an average of 28.7% of body weight, about 71 pounds, and reported substantially less knee pain than placebo.

## Why this trial exists

Knee osteoarthritis is common in people with obesity. Every extra pound adds several pounds of load on the knee during walking, and fat tissue also releases inflammatory signals. Weight loss reduces pain, but usually only modestly, because diet-and-exercise programs lose about 5% to 10% of weight.

A drug that produces 25% or more weight loss tests whether larger losses give larger pain relief.

## Design

- Adults with obesity and knee osteoarthritis.
- Weekly injections of retatrutide or placebo for 68 weeks.
- Dose groups including 9 mg and 12 mg.
- Two main goals: percent weight change and change in knee pain.

## The headline numbers

| Measure | 12 mg result |
|---|---|
| Average weight loss | 28.7% |
| Average pounds | about 71 |
| Duration | 68 weeks |
| Knee pain | Substantial improvement vs placebo |

Placebo participants lost only a small amount of weight, and their pain scores improved less.

## How knee pain was measured

Osteoarthritis trials use questionnaires such as WOMAC, which asks patients to rate pain, stiffness, and physical function in daily activities like climbing stairs. It is a standard tool, and a drop of a certain size is considered clinically meaningful. Full pain data will come with the published paper.

## What the pain result means

It suggests that larger weight loss can bring larger relief, at least in the short to medium term. It does not show that cartilage regrows. Osteoarthritis is structural damage, and weight loss reduces the load and inflammation that make it hurt.

Some participants may need knee replacement anyway. The trial did not report on that.

## Safety in TRIUMPH-4

Most side effects were gastrointestinal. A larger share of people on higher doses reported dysesthesia, an unusual skin sensation such as tingling or sensitivity. More people stopped at higher doses, and Lilly noted that some did so because they had reached a weight where they did not want to lose more.

Full adverse event data is expected in the publication.

## Limits of the result

1. It is a topline press release, not a peer-reviewed paper.
2. The population has obesity and knee osteoarthritis specifically.
3. Sixty-eight weeks does not show long-term safety.
4. The trial does not show effects on joint structure or on needing surgery.

## What comes next

Lilly plans to present full results at a medical meeting and publish them. Regulators will look at TRIUMPH-4 along with the rest of the program, not on its own.

## Availability

Retatrutide is investigational and not available by prescription. It has not been approved for knee osteoarthritis or obesity.

## Weight loss and osteoarthritis: what earlier research shows

Prior studies give context for the TRIUMPH-4 pain result:

- In the IDEA trial, older adults with knee osteoarthritis who combined diet and exercise and lost about 10% of their weight had meaningfully less pain and better function than those who did only one.
- In the STEP 9 trial, semaglutide 2.4 mg produced about 14% weight loss over 68 weeks in adults with obesity and knee osteoarthritis, with a large reduction in WOMAC pain scores compared with placebo.

TRIUMPH-4 pushes further along the same line, with roughly twice the weight loss and, according to Lilly, substantial pain improvement.

## What happens to the knee as weight falls

Each pound lost takes roughly four pounds of load off the knee with each step. Over a day of walking, that adds up to thousands of pounds of force. Lower body fat also means fewer inflammatory chemicals circulating, which may reduce pain independent of load.

## Movement still matters

Trials of weight-loss drugs in osteoarthritis usually encourage exercise. Strengthening the muscles around the knee, especially the quadriceps, improves stability and pain. Because weight-loss drugs can also cost some lean mass, exercise is a way to protect the muscle that supports the joint.

## Questions people ask

### Does this mean retatrutide treats arthritis?

It means weight loss from retatrutide reduced pain in people with obesity and knee osteoarthritis. It has not been shown to repair cartilage or to help osteoarthritis in people without obesity.

### Will it prevent knee replacement?

Not shown. That would need a longer trial with surgery as an outcome.

### What about hip or hand osteoarthritis?

Weight affects hip load similarly, but hand osteoarthritis is less related to weight. TRIUMPH-4 tested knees only.

## Bottom line

TRIUMPH-4 showed that very large weight loss and less knee pain can go together. The weight number is the largest reported in a phase 3 obesity trial so far. Wait for the full paper for safety and pain details.
