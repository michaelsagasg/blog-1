Based on the phase 2 trial curves, losing 30 pounds on retatrutide took most participants about 3 to 6 months, depending on starting weight and dose. Someone starting at 300 lb reaches 30 pounds (10%) faster than someone starting at 180 lb (about 17%).

The percentage of body weight is the useful number, not the pounds.

## Converting 30 pounds to a percentage

| Starting weight | 30 lb as a percentage |
|---|---|
| 180 lb | 16.7% |
| 210 lb | 14.3% |
| 240 lb | 12.5% |
| 270 lb | 11.1% |
| 300 lb | 10.0% |

Phase 2 average losses at the top doses were roughly 17.5% at 24 weeks and 24.2% at 48 weeks. That places 10% to 12% around months 3 to 4 and 15% to 17% near month 6.

## Timeline by starting weight

- **300 lb:** 30 pounds is 10%, so about 3 to 4 months at higher doses.
- **240 lb:** 12.5%, so about 4 months.
- **200 lb:** 15%, so about 5 to 6 months.
- **170 lb:** about 17.6%, close to the 24-week average and possibly past it.

These are averages. Half of participants took longer.

## What limits speed

The dose ladder is the main limit. Trials started low and rose every four weeks, so the strongest effect arrives after month two or three. Skipping ahead to a higher dose was not part of the trials, and it raises the risk of vomiting and dehydration.

## Is faster better?

Not necessarily. Rapid loss increases the chance of:

- Loss of lean mass along with fat
- Gallstones, which are more common with fast weight loss
- Hair shedding, usually temporary
- Dehydration and low blood pressure
- Nutrient shortfalls when eating very little

A rate of about 1% to 2% of body weight per week is a common upper range in obesity guidance. Trial averages fall inside it.

## How to lose 30 pounds while keeping muscle

1. Hit a protein target. Guidance for people losing weight often lands at 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg body weight per day.
2. Lift weights two to three times weekly.
3. Keep calories above a very-low-calorie range unless supervised.
4. Drink fluids, especially when nausea or diarrhea is present.
5. Sleep seven hours or more.

## Plateaus and slow weeks

The average curve is smooth, but individual curves are jagged. Water retention from salt, hormones, or constipation can hide loss for one to two weeks. If the four-week trend is flat, review dose, intake, and side effects with a clinician.

## Who lost less than average

In the trials, people with type 2 diabetes lost less, and so did those who stopped early or reduced the dose. Older adults often lose slightly less. Someone in one of these groups should expect a longer path to 30 pounds.

## Why 30 pounds is not the finish line

Health benefits in the trials, such as lower blood pressure and blood sugar, started with 5% to 10% weight loss and continued to grow with more loss. Keeping the weight off requires continued treatment or strong habits. Related drugs show regain after stopping.

## Availability

Retatrutide is investigational and not approved. The trial timelines describe supervised use of a pharmaceutical product, not products sold online.

## Questions people ask

### Can I speed it up by eating less?

Eating very little on top of a drug that already cuts appetite raises the risk of muscle loss, dizziness, and gallstones. Trials paired the drug with a modest calorie target and lifestyle advice, not crash dieting.

### Does exercise change the number?

Exercise adds a small extra deficit, but its bigger role is preserving muscle and helping keep weight off later.

### What if I lose 30 pounds and want to stop?

Related drugs show regain after stopping, so have a plan for maintenance before you reach the goal.

## Bottom line

For most people, 30 pounds on retatrutide means about 3 to 6 months at higher trial doses. The pace is set by the dose ladder, and a steady, muscle-protecting approach beats a rush.
