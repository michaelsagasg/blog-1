Yes, most of the weight lost on retatrutide in trials was fat, but the main driver is eating less, not a direct fat-burning effect. Retatrutide also acts on the glucagon receptor, which may raise energy expenditure and clear fat from the liver. How much that adds on top of appetite suppression is still being studied.

## How retatrutide is built

It activates three hormone receptors:

- **GLP-1:** slows stomach emptying, signals fullness, lowers appetite, and improves blood sugar.
- **GIP:** supports insulin release and may improve how fat tissue handles energy.
- **Glucagon:** raises energy expenditure and increases fat breakdown in the liver.

Semaglutide activates only the first. Tirzepatide activates the first two. The third receptor is what sets retatrutide apart.

## What trials show about fat versus muscle

In the phase 2 obesity trial, a body composition sub-study used DEXA scans. Most of the weight lost was fat mass, and the rest was lean mass. The proportion was similar to other weight-loss drugs, where roughly a quarter to a third of the lost weight can be lean mass.

Lean mass includes muscle, water, organs, and bone. Some of it is expected to fall as the body gets smaller.

## The liver fat finding

A separate phase 2 sub-study looked at liver fat in people with fatty liver disease. Liver fat fell sharply, and in the higher dose groups a large majority reached normal liver fat by week 24. Liver fat is usually the first fat to go on weight-loss drugs, and the glucagon effect may speed it up.

## Does it raise metabolism?

Glucagon can increase energy expenditure in animal and human studies. But when people lose weight, their metabolism usually drops, since there is less body to power. Whether retatrutide offsets that is unknown. Human studies of energy expenditure with retatrutide are limited.

The cautious answer is that the appetite effect is proven, and the metabolic boost is plausible but unproven at a meaningful scale.

## Why "burning fat" is the wrong frame

To lose fat, you need a sustained energy deficit. Retatrutide creates it mainly by reducing hunger so people eat less without white-knuckling it. The body then draws on stored fat. There is no "melting" switch.

## Signs of fat loss versus muscle loss

- Waist shrinks faster than weight suggests: fat loss.
- Strength drops with weight: muscle loss.
- Weight drops fast in the first week or two: mostly water and gut contents.

A DEXA or bioimpedance scan gives numbers, though bioimpedance is less accurate.

## Protecting muscle while losing fat

1. Lift weights or do resistance training two to three times weekly.
2. Aim for enough protein. Guidance for people losing weight often lands at 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg body weight.
3. Avoid very low calorie diets on top of the drug.
4. Sleep enough and manage stress, since both affect muscle retention.

## Fat loss and the rest of the body

Reducing visceral fat, the fat around organs, is linked to lower blood pressure, better blood sugar, and lower triglycerides. Phase 2 reported improvements in each.

## Availability

Retatrutide is investigational and unapproved. These findings come from trial participants receiving a pharmaceutical product. They cannot be applied to unverified products.

## What the trials say about energy expenditure

Glucagon is known to raise energy expenditure in short studies, which is the reason it was added to the design. In practice, the effect in people on retatrutide has been hard to separate from the drop in body weight. Some trial analyses estimated how much of the weight loss exceeded what appetite reduction alone would predict, and the results suggested a contribution from the third receptor, but the size is debated.

## Heart rate as a clue

The rise in resting heart rate seen in phase 2, peaking around week 24, is one visible sign of glucagon and GLP-1 activity on the body. It is not a measure of fat burning, and it is a reason to monitor anyone with heart conditions.

## What the urine and breath tell you

People sometimes ask whether smelly urine or a metallic taste means fat is burning. Ketones can appear when calorie intake is low, but they are not a reliable gauge of fat loss, and they do not mean the drug is working better. Another post covers urine changes in detail.

## Fat loss timeline in the trials

Waist circumference fell steadily through the 48-week phase 2 trial. Liver fat fell earliest, followed by belly fat and body weight. Triglycerides dropped along with these. The order fits what other drugs in the class show: metabolic markers improve before the scale reaches its final number.

## Bottom line

Retatrutide produces mostly fat loss, mainly through appetite suppression, with a glucagon effect that may add fat burning and clear liver fat. Protect muscle with resistance training and protein.
