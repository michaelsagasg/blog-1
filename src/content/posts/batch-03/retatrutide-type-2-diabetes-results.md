In a 24-week phase 2 trial in adults with type 2 diabetes, retatrutide lowered HbA1c by up to about 2 percentage points and reduced body weight by up to about 17%. Both effects were larger than with dulaglutide, an approved GLP-1 drug used as a comparator.

The trial was published in The Lancet in 2023.

## Trial design

- Adults with type 2 diabetes, mostly treated with metformin.
- Randomized to weekly retatrutide at several doses, dulaglutide 1.5 mg, or placebo.
- 24 weeks of treatment.
- Primary outcome: change in HbA1c at 24 weeks.

## What HbA1c means

HbA1c reflects average blood sugar over about three months. In type 2 diabetes, a common treatment target is below 7%. A drop of 1 point is a meaningful improvement, and 2 points is large.

## Results

| Measure | Retatrutide (highest doses) | Dulaglutide 1.5 mg |
|---|---|---|
| HbA1c change | about -2.0 points | about -1.4 points |
| Weight change | about -17% | about -3% |

The largest effects were at the higher doses. Exact numbers vary by dose group, so check the paper for details.

## Reaching targets

A large share of people on higher retatrutide doses reached an HbA1c below 7%, and many reached below 5.7%, the normal range. That suggests some participants moved into non-diabetic glucose levels while on treatment, though it is not remission unless it lasts after stopping.

## Weight loss in diabetes

Weight loss was smaller than in people without diabetes, which follows the pattern for all drugs in this class. In phase 2 obesity, 12 mg reached 24.2% at 48 weeks, while the diabetes trial reported about 17% at 24 weeks. The trials have different durations, so the gap is not all due to diabetes.

## Safety

Findings mirrored the obesity trial:

- Nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea, mostly early and during dose increases
- Rise in resting heart rate
- Low blood sugar was rare, since the drug acts in a glucose-dependent way, though risk rises when combined with insulin or sulfonylureas

## What phase 3 will add

TRANSCEND-T2D trials and TRIUMPH-2 test retatrutide in larger groups with type 2 diabetes, against placebo and active comparators such as semaglutide. They will show:

- Long-term glucose control
- Effects on kidney function
- Effects on cardiovascular risk
- Comparisons with other treatments

## What this means for people with diabetes

Retatrutide looks powerful for both blood sugar and weight, but it is not approved. Approved options such as semaglutide and tirzepatide already offer large benefits. Anyone with diabetes should not adjust medicines on their own, especially insulin, since weight loss and glucose changes can lower needs and raise the risk of low blood sugar.

## Questions

### Could it cure diabetes?

Type 2 diabetes can go into remission with large weight loss, but it can return. Trials measure glucose control while on treatment, not cure.

### Does it replace metformin?

No such data exists. Trial participants generally continued their background medicine.

## Availability

Retatrutide is investigational and not approved for diabetes or obesity. Anyone with diabetes considering unverified products risks unpredictable blood sugar effects.

## Comparison with tirzepatide in diabetes

Tirzepatide's SURPASS program reported HbA1c reductions of about 2 points and weight loss of about 7 to 12 kg in type 2 diabetes over 40 weeks. Retatrutide's phase 2 numbers look at least as large after a shorter 24 weeks, though phase 2 trials are small and cannot be compared directly with phase 3 programs.

## Who should watch these trials

- People with type 2 diabetes who need major weight loss and better glucose control.
- People with diabetes and fatty liver disease, since retatrutide reduced liver fat.
- People with diabetes and kidney disease, who will look to outcomes data.

## Safety points specific to diabetes

Diabetic eye disease can worsen temporarily when blood sugar falls quickly, a warning seen with semaglutide. People with retinopathy need eye monitoring. Low blood sugar risk rises with insulin or sulfonylureas, and doses of those medicines often need to be reduced. Dehydration from vomiting and diarrhea can strain the kidneys.

## Bottom line

Retatrutide lowered HbA1c by about 2 points and weight by about 17% in 24 weeks in type 2 diabetes. Phase 3 will show whether those results hold and how it compares with existing drugs.
