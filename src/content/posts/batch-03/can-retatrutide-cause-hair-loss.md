Yes, hair shedding can occur during retatrutide treatment, most likely because of rapid weight loss and reduced nutrient intake rather than a direct drug effect. The usual form is temporary and starts about two to four months after weight begins to drop.

Semaglutide and tirzepatide labels list hair loss among reported effects, and the same is expected here. Retatrutide trials have not reported hair loss as a leading adverse event in public summaries, but full data is still coming.

## What is happening

The most common cause is telogen effluvium. Hair follicles cycle between growth and rest. A physical stress, such as fast weight loss, surgery, illness, or a crash diet, pushes more follicles into the resting phase at once. About two to four months later, those hairs shed.

This is diffuse thinning across the scalp, not bald patches. It rarely causes full baldness.

## Risk factors

- Rapid weight loss, especially more than 1% to 2% of body weight per week
- Low protein intake
- Iron, zinc, biotin, or vitamin D deficiency
- Very low calorie intake
- Thyroid disease
- Postpartum status or recent illness
- Genetic hair thinning that becomes visible as the face slims

Women report it more often, though men are affected too.

## Timeline

| Time after weight loss starts | What happens |
|---|---|
| Weeks 0 to 8 | Nothing visible |
| Months 2 to 4 | Increased shedding in the shower and on brushes |
| Months 4 to 6 | Shedding usually peaks and then slows |
| Months 6 to 12 | Regrowth begins, usually full by a year |

If shedding continues past six months or leaves bald patches, get it checked.

## What may help

1. **Get enough protein.** Aim for the range in weight-loss guidance, around 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg body weight.
2. **Check iron and vitamin D.** Ask for ferritin, vitamin D, thyroid, and B12 tests, and correct deficiencies.
3. **Slow the rate of loss** if it is very fast, by discussing dose pacing.
4. **Eat enough overall.** Very low calorie intake is a strong trigger.
5. **Be gentle.** Avoid tight hairstyles and harsh treatments.
6. **Be patient.** It usually resolves without treatment.

## What does not work

Biotin megadoses have little evidence unless you are deficient, and they can interfere with lab tests. Expensive supplements marketed for hair rarely outperform correcting real deficiencies. Minoxidil can help some hair loss types but is not a fix for telogen effluvium and needs a clinician's advice.

## When to see a clinician

- Bald patches or scalp irritation
- Shedding past six months
- Signs of thyroid disease, such as fatigue, cold intolerance, or weight changes not explained by the drug
- Hair loss with other symptoms such as rash or joint pain

## Reassurance

For most people, the hair grows back once weight stabilizes and nutrition is adequate. It is upsetting, but it is a sign of the body adjusting, not of permanent damage.

## Availability

Retatrutide is investigational and unapproved. Hair loss data will come in full trial publications, and this post relies on knowledge of rapid weight loss and related drugs.

## Other causes to rule out

Hair loss during weight loss can have more than one cause at once. Check for:

- **Thyroid problems,** which are common and treatable.
- **Iron deficiency,** especially in menstruating women. Ferritin below about 30 ng/mL is often linked to shedding.
- **Low vitamin D and B12.**
- **Medications,** such as some blood pressure drugs, antidepressants, and blood thinners.
- **Hormonal conditions,** such as PCOS or menopause.
- **Scalp conditions,** such as dandruff or fungal infection.

A clinician can run a few blood tests to sort these out.

## Male and female pattern thinning

Genetic thinning can become more noticeable when the face slims or when hair is styled differently. It is not caused by the drug. If thinning is concentrated at the temples, crown, or part line, it may be pattern loss that would have happened anyway, and treatments exist.

## What the data says about GLP-1 drugs

Hair loss appeared as a reported adverse event in semaglutide and tirzepatide trials, at rates of a few percent, higher in the weight-loss trials than in diabetes trials, and higher in women. That fits the rapid weight loss explanation. The drug does not appear to act on hair follicles directly.

## Bottom line

Temporary shedding is plausible on retatrutide, driven mainly by fast weight loss and low nutrient intake. Protein, checking for deficiencies, and patience are the practical steps.
