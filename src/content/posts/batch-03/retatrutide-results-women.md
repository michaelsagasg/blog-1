Women were a large share of the retatrutide trial participants, and trial results are reported for everyone combined. Detailed breakdowns by sex have been limited in the public data so far. For the related drugs semaglutide and tirzepatide, women on average lose a slightly larger percentage of body weight than men.

## Who was in the trials

Weight-loss trials in this drug class typically enroll a large share of women. That means results for women are well represented in the average.

The 24.2% average at the top dose therefore includes many women, though the exact split by sex has not been the focus of the reports.

## What other drugs show

Subgroup analyses for semaglutide and tirzepatide found that:

- Women lost a somewhat higher percentage of body weight than men on the same dose.
- Men often lost more total pounds, because they start heavier on average.
- Both groups responded strongly compared with placebo.

Retatrutide has not yet published a full sex-based analysis, so it is fair to expect a similar pattern but not to assume it.

## Body composition and sex

Women generally carry more fat mass and less muscle than men at the same weight. In trials of weight-loss drugs, most of the weight lost is fat, but some lean mass is lost too. Protecting muscle matters for everyone, and it may matter more for women after menopause, when muscle and bone loss speed up.

Practical steps that trials of this drug class encourage:

- Eat adequate protein.
- Do resistance training two to three times a week.
- Get enough calcium and vitamin D.

## Menopause and hormones

Weight gain often rises around menopause, and some women ask whether these drugs work the same way then. Trials included postmenopausal women, and they did lose weight. There is no signal in the public data that menopause blocks the effect, but dedicated studies are limited.

## Fertility and pregnancy

This is one area where the guidance is firm for the drug class. Semaglutide and tirzepatide labels advise against use during pregnancy, and people planning a pregnancy are told to stop before trying to conceive. Retatrutide trials excluded pregnant and breastfeeding women. There is no safety data in pregnancy.

Weight loss can also improve ovulation in some women, including those with polycystic ovary syndrome. Tirzepatide's label notes that oral contraceptive absorption may be reduced after starting and after dose increases, and it advises a backup method. Whether retatrutide does the same is unknown, but the class effect on gastric emptying suggests asking a clinician.

## Side effects by sex

Women reported stomach side effects such as nausea somewhat more often in trials of related drugs. Retatrutide's public data does not separate these yet.

## Hair loss and rapid weight loss

Hair shedding after fast weight loss affects women more often in reports. It is usually temporary. A separate post covers that topic.

## Availability

Retatrutide is investigational and not prescribed. Women considering any weight-loss drug should talk with a clinician who knows their history, especially around pregnancy plans and medicines they already take.

## Sex differences in appetite and side effects

Small studies suggest women may report stronger appetite reduction and more nausea on GLP-1-based drugs. The reasons are not fully understood. Body size plays a part, since women are on average smaller and receive the same fixed dose as larger men. A fixed dose means a higher exposure per kilogram for a smaller person.

That is one reason clinicians raise doses gradually and adjust for side effects rather than pushing to the top dose.

## Questions women ask

### Will it affect my period?

Rapid weight loss and calorie restriction can change cycles for any drug or diet. Some women see cycles become more regular, especially with PCOS, and others see delays. Report unexpected changes to a clinician.

### Does it cause bone loss?

Weight loss of any kind reduces bone density somewhat. In tirzepatide trials, the effect was in line with the amount of weight lost. Weight-bearing exercise and adequate calcium, vitamin D, and protein help. Postmenopausal women should ask about bone density scans.

### Is hair loss more common?

Hair shedding is reported more often by women after rapid weight loss, likely because the body diverts resources from hair growth for a few months. It usually resolves after weight stabilizes.

### What about gallbladder problems?

Gallstones are more common with fast weight loss, and women are at higher baseline risk. Pain in the upper right abdomen, especially after meals, deserves medical attention.

## What trials still need to report

For retatrutide, useful information would include:

- Weight loss by sex at each dose.
- Side effect rates by sex.
- Results in pre- and postmenopausal groups.
- Any effect on contraception and pregnancy planning.

Until then, the data on women comes mostly from the overall averages and from the related drugs.

## Bottom line

Women made up a substantial share of the trial participants, so the headline results reflect them. Expect similar or slightly higher percentage weight loss than in men, based on related drugs, and wait for published subgroup data to confirm.
