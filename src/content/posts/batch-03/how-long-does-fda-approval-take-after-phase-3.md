After phase 3 data is ready, FDA approval usually takes about 10 to 12 months from the date a company submits its application. Priority review can cut that to about 6 to 8 months. Add the months a company needs to prepare the filing, and the total is often a year to a year and a half after the key data.

## The steps

1. **Finish the key trials and analyze the data.** This can take several months.
2. **Prepare the application.** For a new drug, it is a New Drug Application (NDA), a very large package covering chemistry, manufacturing, animal and human data.
3. **Submit to the FDA.** The FDA has 60 days to decide whether to accept the filing for review.
4. **Review.** Under PDUFA rules, standard review takes 10 months after the 60-day filing period for new molecular entities, so about 12 months from submission. Priority review takes 6 months after filing, so about 8 months.
5. **Decision.** Approval, or a complete response letter that lists issues to fix.
6. **Launch.** Manufacturing, pricing, and insurance coverage decide when patients can get the drug.

## Faster paths

- **Priority review** for drugs that offer significant improvement.
- **Fast track and breakthrough designations,** which allow more frequent FDA contact and rolling submissions.
- **Priority review vouchers,** which a company can use to speed a review.
- **National priority vouchers,** a newer FDA program that promises reviews of one to two months for certain products.

Whether retatrutide qualifies for any of these depends on decisions by Lilly and the FDA.

## Examples from the same class

Tirzepatide's SURMOUNT-1 obesity results came out in mid-2022. Lilly submitted the application later that year, and the FDA approved Zepbound in November 2023. That is a bit over a year from data to approval. Semaglutide 2.4 mg followed a similar timeline, with approval in 2021 after STEP data in 2020 and 2021.

## What can extend the timeline

- **Manufacturing inspections.** Plant issues have delayed approvals of other drugs.
- **Requests for more data.** Major amendments can add three months.
- **Advisory committee meetings.** These add scheduling time.
- **Complete response letters.** A rejection means resubmission and a new review.

## What happens after approval

The company sets a launch date, negotiates coverage with insurers, and ships product. Supply can be limited at first. Both semaglutide and tirzepatide faced shortages after launch. Approval does not mean everyone who wants it can get it right away.

## Outside the US

The European Medicines Agency typically takes about a year to review. Other countries follow their own schedules and often start after the FDA or EMA.

## What it means for retatrutide

Retatrutide has one phase 3 result. Even if the rest of the program reports well, a filing, review, and launch would put availability at least a year or more after the final key data. Those dates are guesses, and delays are common.

## Common questions

### Can a drug be sold while the FDA reviews it?

No. Until approval, a drug cannot be marketed in the US. Compounded or research-labeled versions sold online are not FDA-approved, and the agency has warned about unapproved GLP-1 products.

### Does approval mean it will be covered by insurance?

Not automatically. Insurers decide coverage after approval, and many plans exclude weight-loss drugs. Semaglutide and tirzepatide coverage for obesity has varied widely.

### Does the FDA always follow its advisory committee?

Not always, but it usually does. Many drugs are approved without a committee meeting.

### What does a complete response letter mean?

It means the FDA is not approving the application yet and lists problems. It might ask for more data, changes to manufacturing, or new trials. Companies often resubmit after addressing them.

### Can approval happen on one trial?

Sometimes, if the trial is large and convincing and other evidence supports it. Weight-loss drugs usually have at least two large trials, though.

## Bottom line

Expect roughly a year of FDA review after filing, plus months of preparation, so at least a year or two after phase 3 finishes.
