Tirzepatide holds an MHRA licence in the UK, sold under the brand name Mounjaro for weight management and type 2 diabetes in eligible adults, available through a prescription from a registered pharmacy. Separately, it is also sold unbranded as a research-grade peptide by specialist online sellers, a market that sits outside the licensed medicines system entirely. These are two different regulatory situations under the same drug name.

## The licensed route

Mounjaro went through Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT and SURPASS trial programmes and received MHRA approval, which means it can be legally prescribed and dispensed through a UK pharmacy following an assessment by a registered prescriber. This is the route with full regulatory oversight, from manufacturing standards to pharmacist dispensing.

## The peptide-seller route

Outside the licensed pathway, tirzepatide is also listed by online sellers as a research chemical or peptide, generally labelled for research use rather than for human consumption. This labelling reflects that this specific supply chain has not gone through the pharmacy dispensing process, even though the active compound has an approved medicine elsewhere.

## Why the same drug has two different sales channels

A medicines licence covers a specific formulation, manufacturer and supply chain, not just the underlying molecule. Mounjaro pens are licensed as a complete product, including manufacturing and quality standards reviewed by the MHRA. A peptide vial sold by a different supplier, even containing the same compound, has not gone through that same product-specific review, which is why it is sold under different terms.

## What this means for a buyer choosing between routes

The prescription route comes with a prescriber's assessment and pharmacy-level oversight built in. The peptide-seller route puts more responsibility on the buyer to evaluate the seller directly, since no regulator has reviewed that specific supply chain. That is why documentation, like a batch-specific certificate of analysis, matters so much more in that market.

## Regulatory guidance can change

Rules around supply, classification and enforcement can shift over time as regulators respond to how a market develops. For the current, authoritative position on buying, importing or possessing tirzepatide outside the licensed pharmacy route, gov.uk and the MHRA are the primary sources to check directly, rather than relying on any single article.

## What tirzepatide does

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. That trial data is what supported its MHRA approval as Mounjaro, and it is a separate question from whether any individual peptide-seller listing is trustworthy.
