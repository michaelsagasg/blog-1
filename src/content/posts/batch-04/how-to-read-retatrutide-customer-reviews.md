The most useful retatrutide reviews are specific ones that mention delivery time, packaging condition, COA accuracy or communication with the seller, not just a high star rating on its own. A five-star average tells you very little if the reviews behind it are generic, clustered together in time, or impossible to verify.

## What a genuine review usually looks like

Genuine reviews tend to mention concrete details: "arrived the next day, cold pack still cold," "batch number matched the COA on the site," or "asked a question on WhatsApp and got a reply within the hour." These specifics are harder to fabricate convincingly at scale than a generic "great product, fast shipping" comment.

## Warning signs in a review section

- **A burst of reviews posted within days of each other**, especially if the seller or product is otherwise new.
- **Near-identical phrasing across multiple reviews**, suggesting a template or incentivised batch.
- **No negative or mixed reviews at all.** Even well-run sellers occasionally have a delayed parcel or a customer service hiccup; an entirely spotless record over a long period is unusual.
- **Reviews with no detail beyond a star rating**, which are easy to generate but tell you little.

## Where to check reviews

Look at reviews on the seller's own site, but also check any independent review platform they link to, such as Trustpilot, since third-party platforms are harder for a single seller to fully control. Cross-referencing between the two gives a more complete picture than either alone.

## What reviews cannot tell you

Reviews reflect other buyers' experience with delivery, communication and packaging, not an independent lab assessment of what was actually in the vial. Even a seller with excellent reviews should still be judged on whether they publish a batch-specific COA, since reviews and product verification answer different questions.

## Reading between the lines on vague praise

A review that says "great product, will buy again" with nothing else gives you no way to judge what "great" actually means to that person. Reviews that explain what specifically went well or wrong are far more useful, even if there are fewer of them.

## Weighing volume against detail

A seller with 50 detailed, varied reviews mentioning specific delivery times and experiences is generally a stronger signal than one with 500 near-identical five-star comments posted in a short window. Volume alone is not a substitute for credibility.

## Using reviews alongside the rest of your checklist

Reviews are one part of evaluating a seller, alongside COA transparency, company details, price per mg and delivery terms. A seller who is strong on documentation but has a handful of mixed reviews about delivery speed is often still a reasonable choice; a seller with glowing reviews but no COA at all is not.
