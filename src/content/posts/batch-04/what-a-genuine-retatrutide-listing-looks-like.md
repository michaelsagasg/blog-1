A genuine retatrutide listing shows four things clearly on the product page: a batch-specific certificate of analysis, real UK company details, a price per mg in line with the rest of the market, and written delivery and returns terms. If a listing is missing more than one of these, treat that as a reason to look elsewhere rather than assume the details exist somewhere else on the site.

## The certificate of analysis

Look for a COA displayed directly on the product page, not something you have to email and request. It should show identity, purity and concentration results, and carry a batch number that matches the one printed on the vial you receive. A generic report reused across every batch is not the same thing as a genuine one.

## Company details that hold up to scrutiny

A genuine listing names the business behind it: a company name, a UK address or company number, and more than one way to get in touch, such as email plus a messaging app or phone line. You should be able to find this information without digging, typically in the footer or an About page.

## Pricing that fits the market

Calculate the price per mg by dividing the listed price by the vial size. Genuine listings tend to sit within a reasonable range of other UK sellers, roughly £3.50 to £6 per mg at the time of writing. A price dramatically below that range is more often a sign of cut corners than a real bargain.

## Delivery and storage information

A genuine listing states its dispatch cutoff, delivery method and expected timeframe, and mentions how the product is kept cool in transit, since retatrutide typically needs refrigeration. Vague shipping information, or none at all, is a gap worth asking about before you order.

## What the product images tell you

Real product photography, showing the actual vial, label and batch number, is a better sign than generic stock photography that could belong to any seller. Some genuine sellers also photograph the COA itself alongside the product.

## Reviews that read like real experiences

Genuine listings tend to have a spread of reviews mentioning specific details, delivery time, packaging, communication, rather than a cluster of near-identical five-star reviews posted within days of each other. Cross-check reviews on the seller's own site against any independent platform they link to.

## A worked example of a genuine listing

Use a listing like this as a benchmark when assessing a seller you are less familiar with.

## Putting the checklist together

| Element | What genuine looks like | What to be wary of |

| Company details | Named business, UK address, multiple contacts | Contact form only |
| Price per mg | In line with market range | Far below every comparable seller |
| Delivery | Stated cutoff, tracked, cold-chain mentioned | Vague or absent |

## What retatrutide is

Retatrutide remains an investigational drug, activating the GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors, still in Phase 3 trials with no UK medicines licence. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. Because it sits outside the licensed pharmacy system, the listing itself is the main source of assurance a buyer has, which is exactly why these four elements matter so much.

## Bottom line

A genuine retatrutide listing is transparent by default: a batch-specific COA, real company details, a fair price per mg, and clear delivery terms, all visible without having to ask. Use this checklist against any UK seller you are considering before you order.
