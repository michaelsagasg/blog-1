Price per mg is the fairest way to compare tirzepatide peptide listings, since vial sizes vary widely between sellers. UK listings typically work out to somewhere between £3.30 and £5 per mg, depending on vial size, seller and current market demand.

## How to calculate it

Price per mg = listed price divided by vial size in mg.

A 30 mg vial priced at £140 works out to about £4.67 per mg. A 60 mg vial priced at £210 works out to £3.50 per mg. This lets you compare listings fairly, even when the total prices look very different.

## Why the total price alone is misleading

A 60 mg vial at £210 looks like the pricier option next to a 10 mg vial at £45. But the 60 mg vial works out to £3.50 per mg, while the 10 mg vial is £4.50 per mg. The larger vial is the better rate despite the higher upfront cost.

## Why larger vials tend to price lower per mg

Fixed costs like packaging, testing and delivery do not scale directly with vial size, so they get spread thinner across a larger vial. This is a general pattern, not a rule every seller follows, which is why calculating the figure yourself matters more than assuming it.

## When a low price per mg deserves more scrutiny, not less

A price per mg well below the rest of the UK market can indicate skipped batch testing, weaker sourcing, or a listing that will not fulfil the order at all. Weigh an unusually low price against the presence of a COA and clear company details before treating it as a genuine deal.

## What tirzepatide is

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As Mounjaro, it is a licensed UK medicine for weight management and type 2 diabetes. As a research-grade peptide, the price you pay covers a product sold outside that licensed pathway, and no price point changes that.

## Checking prices periodically

Because peptide pricing is not fixed by regulation, it moves with sourcing costs, demand and competition among sellers. A price comparison from several months ago may no longer reflect the current market, so it is worth rechecking before a repeat order.

## Bottom line

Always divide price by vial size in mg before comparing tirzepatide listings. Expect a range of roughly £3.30 to £5 per mg across UK sellers, treat unusually low figures with more caution, and pair the number with COA and company checks before you order.
