A fair retatrutide return policy should cover, in writing, what happens if a parcel arrives damaged, arrives significantly late, or does not match what was ordered, with a clear timeframe for reporting issues and a defined process for a refund or replacement. If a seller's policy is vague or exists only as a verbal promise, you have little to rely on if something goes wrong.

## The minimum a written policy should include

- **A reporting window.** How many days after delivery you have to raise an issue, commonly 7 to 14 days for this type of product.
- **What counts as a valid claim.** Damaged packaging, a broken vial, missing items, or a product that does not match the batch shown on the COA.
- **What the seller will do.** A replacement, a refund, or both, and how long that typically takes to process.
- **Contact method for raising a claim.** A specific email address or form, not just a general enquiry line.

## Why this matters more for peptides than for many products

Retatrutide is not a licensed medicine, so there is no pharmacy-level complaints or safety process standing behind a purchase the way there might be for a dispensed medicine. The seller's own written policy is the main mechanism you have if a parcel is damaged, delayed or does not match what was promised.

## What "damaged" should cover

Because retatrutide typically needs refrigeration, a fair policy should also address temperature-related issues, such as a cold pack that failed in transit during hot weather, not just physical breakage. A policy silent on this leaves a real gap for a temperature-sensitive product.

## Refund versus replacement

Some sellers offer only a replacement, others offer a refund, and better policies give the buyer a choice. A replacement makes sense if you still want the product and simply received a faulty batch. A refund makes more sense if the issue has made you reconsider the seller or the order entirely.

## What a fair policy does not need to include

A returns policy for a product like this does not need to accept a simple change of mind once a vial has shipped, given the nature of a temperature-sensitive, single-use type of product. A fair policy focuses on the seller's responsibility for a genuine fault, not a general right to return an opened order for any reason.

## Checking the policy before you order, not after

Look for the returns and delivery policy on the seller's site before you place an order, not only when you need it. If you cannot find one, or it is buried behind vague language like "contact us for details," treat that as a gap worth asking about directly.

## What good practice looks like

Enzo Meds is one example of a UK seller publishing clear delivery and support channels, including a stated process for order issues via direct contact methods like WhatsApp or email. Use a policy this accessible as your benchmark when reviewing a less transparent seller.

## What to keep as evidence

Keep the order confirmation, delivery photos of any damage, and the COA you were shown at the time of ordering. This documentation makes any claim faster to resolve and gives you a clear record if a dispute needs to go further, such as through your card provider.

## Bottom line

A fair retatrutide return policy is written down, covers damage including temperature-related issues, states a clear reporting window, and explains whether you will get a refund or replacement. Check for this before you order, and treat its absence as a real gap in an otherwise good-looking listing.
