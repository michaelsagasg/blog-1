Comparing UK retatrutide suppliers works best as a side-by-side scorecard on four points: COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details. Open two or three listings at once and score each one the same way, rather than judging them one at a time from memory.

## Set up the comparison

Pick two or three UK sellers you are considering and open their retatrutide product pages in separate tabs. For each one, note down: whether a COA is shown, the vial size and price, the delivery terms, and the company details listed.

## Point 1: COA transparency

Check whether each seller shows a certificate of analysis on the product page itself, and whether the batch number on it is specific rather than generic. A seller that shows this upfront scores higher than one where you would have to ask.

## Point 2: Price per mg

Divide each seller's price by their vial size in mg. A supplier listing a 10 mg vial at £45 works out to £4.50 per mg. Another listing a 15 mg vial at £58 works out to about £3.87 per mg, a better rate despite the higher total price. Compare this figure, not the sticker price.

## Point 3: Delivery terms

Note the stated dispatch cutoff, delivery method, and whether cold-chain packaging is mentioned, since retatrutide typically needs refrigeration. A supplier offering next-day tracked delivery with insulated packaging scores higher than one with vague or missing shipping information.

## Point 4: Company details

Check for a UK business name, address or company number, and more than one contact method. A supplier that is easy to identify and contact scores higher than an anonymous storefront.

## A sample comparison table

| Supplier | COA shown | Price per mg | Delivery | Company details |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seller A | Yes, batch-specific | £4.50 | Next-day tracked, insulated | UK company name and address |

In this example, Seller B's lower price is offset by weaker transparency on every other point, which makes Sellers A and C the stronger choices despite costing more.

## Weighing the four points together

No single point should override the others automatically. A great price with no COA is a risk. A strong COA at a very high price compared to the rest of the market is worth questioning too. The strongest suppliers tend to score well across all four points at once, not just one.

## What you are comparing suppliers on

Retatrutide remains an investigational compound, still in Phase 3 trials and without a UK medicines licence. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. Because there is no pharmacy oversight for this product, the comparison you do yourself is the main check standing between you and a poor-quality or non-existent product.

## Bottom line

Score UK retatrutide suppliers side by side on COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details, using a table if it helps. A supplier that is strong across all four is a safer and often better-value choice than one that wins only on price.
