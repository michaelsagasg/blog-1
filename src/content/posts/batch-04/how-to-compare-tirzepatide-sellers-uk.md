Comparing UK tirzepatide sellers works best as a side-by-side scorecard across four points: COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details. This applies to the research-grade peptide market; comparing prescription routes for Mounjaro is a separate process involving prescriber assessments rather than product listings.

## Setting up the comparison

Shortlist two or three UK sellers and open their tirzepatide listings together. For each one, note whether a COA is shown, the vial size and price, the delivery terms, and the company details on the page.

## Point 1: COA transparency

Check whether each seller displays a certificate of analysis directly on the product page, and whether it references a specific batch number rather than a generic report. A seller that shows this without being asked scores higher.

## Point 2: Price per mg

Divide each seller's price by their vial size. A seller listing a 30 mg vial at £140 works out to about £4.67 per mg. Another listing a 60 mg vial at £210 works out to £3.50 per mg, a meaningfully better rate despite the higher total cost. Always compare this figure rather than the sticker price.

## Point 3: Delivery terms

Note the stated dispatch cutoff, delivery method and whether cold-chain packaging is mentioned, since tirzepatide vials generally need refrigeration. A seller offering next-day tracked delivery with insulated packaging scores higher than one silent on storage.

## Point 4: Company details

Check for a UK business name, address or company number, and more than one way to make contact. Easier to identify and reach means easier to hold accountable if something goes wrong.

| Seller | COA shown | Price per mg | Delivery | Company details |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seller A | Yes, batch-specific | £4.67 | Next-day tracked, insulated | UK company name and address |

Here, Seller B's lower price comes with weaker transparency on every other point, making Sellers A and C the more reliable options despite the higher cost.

## Weighing the four points together

None of the four should dominate on its own. A low price with no COA is a risk, and an unusually high price relative to the rest of the market is also worth questioning. The strongest sellers score well across all four points together, not just one.

## What you are comparing

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As a research-grade peptide sold outside the licensed pharmacy route, there is no regulator checking the specific supply chain on your behalf, which is why doing this comparison yourself matters so much.

## Bottom line

Score UK tirzepatide sellers side by side on COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details. A seller strong across all four is a more reliable choice than one that only competes on price.
