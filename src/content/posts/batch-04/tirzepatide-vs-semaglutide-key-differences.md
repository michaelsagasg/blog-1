Tirzepatide and semaglutide differ mainly in receptor targets and trial results. Tirzepatide activates both the GLP-1 and GIP receptors, while semaglutide activates GLP-1 alone. In head-to-head and cross-trial comparisons, tirzepatide has generally shown a higher average weight loss, though both are licensed UK medicines with established trial records.

## Receptor targets

Semaglutide is a single-receptor drug, acting only on GLP-1, the gut hormone that slows stomach emptying, reduces appetite and supports insulin release after eating. Tirzepatide adds GIP receptor activity alongside GLP-1, which research suggests enhances the overall metabolic effect compared with targeting GLP-1 alone.

## Trial results compared

| Drug | Trial | Average weight loss | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semaglutide | STEP trials | About 15% | 68 weeks |
| Tirzepatide | SURMOUNT-1 | About 21% (highest dose) | 72 weeks |

These are company-reported averages from specific trial populations over defined periods, not individual guarantees, and the two trial programmes involved different populations and designs, so the comparison is informative but not a precise like-for-like measure.

## UK licensing and brand names

Semaglutide is licensed in the UK as Wegovy for weight management and Ozempic for type 2 diabetes. Tirzepatide is licensed as Mounjaro for both weight management and type 2 diabetes in eligible adults. Both require a prescription through a registered pharmacy following an assessment by a prescriber.

## Dosing schedule

Both drugs are dosed once weekly by injection. Semaglutide has a half-life of about seven days; tirzepatide's is about five days. Both are similar enough in pharmacokinetics to support a weekly schedule, with each following its own staged dose-escalation protocol to manage tolerability.

## Side effect profile

Both drugs share a broadly similar side effect profile dominated by gastrointestinal symptoms, nausea, diarrhoea, constipation and vomiting, particularly during dose escalation. Neither drug's side effect profile is dramatically different from the other in kind, though individual tolerability varies.

## Why tirzepatide's trial results are generally higher

The added GIP receptor activity is the leading mechanistic explanation researchers point to for tirzepatide's generally higher average weight loss compared with semaglutide across separate trial programmes. This is an area of ongoing research interest, and it is part of why triple-agonist drugs like retatrutide, which add glucagon receptor activity on top of GLP-1 and GIP, have drawn further attention. ## What neither drug guarantees

Trial averages reflect a study population over a defined period, not a promise about any specific individual's result. Response to either drug varies considerably between people, and neither drug's trial data should be read as predicting a particular personal outcome.

## Which one is right for a given person

That is a clinical decision involving eligibility, medical history and individual circumstances, made together with a prescriber during an assessment, not something a general comparison article can determine.

## Bottom line

Tirzepatide targets GLP-1 and GIP, while semaglutide targets GLP-1 alone, and tirzepatide has generally shown higher average weight loss across separate trial programmes, an effect researchers largely attribute to the added GIP receptor activity. Both are licensed UK medicines, dosed weekly, with broadly similar gastrointestinal side effect profiles.
