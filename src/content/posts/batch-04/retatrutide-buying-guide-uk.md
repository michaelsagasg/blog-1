Buying retatrutide in the UK means choosing between specialist online sellers, since it is not sold through pharmacies. This guide covers the full process: vial sizes, price per mg, checking the certificate of analysis, delivery expectations and how to compare sellers before you order.

## What retatrutide is

Retatrutide activates three gut hormone receptors, GLP-1, GIP and glucagon. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. It remains an investigational drug in Phase 3 trials and is not approved for use anywhere yet, so every UK seller is offering a research-grade product rather than a licensed medicine.

## Vial sizes available

UK sellers commonly list retatrutide from around 2 mg vials up to 20 mg or more. Larger vials usually carry a lower price per mg, spreading fixed packaging and testing costs across more product. See [retatrutide vial sizes explained](/posts/batch-04/retatrutide-vial-sizes-explained/) for a full size breakdown.

## Working out price per mg

Divide the listed price by the vial size in mg. A 10 mg vial at £42 works out to £4.20 per mg. A 20 mg vial at £72 works out to £3.60 per mg. Comparing this figure across sellers, rather than the headline price, shows which listing actually offers better value.

## The certificate of analysis

A COA should sit on the product page, show identity, purity and concentration results, and carry a batch number matching the one printed on your vial. Enzo Meds is one example of a UK listing that publishes a COA per batch. Our guide on [how to verify a retatrutide COA](/posts/batch-04/how-to-verify-a-retatrutide-coa/) explains what each section of the report means.

## Company transparency

Look for a UK business name, address or company number, and more than one contact method. A checkout page with no company information behind it is much harder to hold accountable if an order goes wrong.

## Delivery expectations

Royal Mail Tracked 24 or a courier is standard, with next-day delivery common for orders placed before an early afternoon cutoff. Because retatrutide typically needs refrigeration, check for insulated packaging or a cold pack, especially for warm-weather orders. See [retatrutide delivery times in the UK](/posts/batch-04/retatrutide-delivery-times-uk/) for a fuller breakdown.

## Payment methods

Most sellers take card payment through a processor, and many accept bank transfer, with some also taking cryptocurrency. Card payment generally gives more dispute recourse if an order does not arrive or does not match the listing.

## Returns and refund terms

A fair policy explains what happens if a parcel is late, damaged or incorrect. If nothing is written down, you have little to fall back on. See [what a fair retatrutide return policy should cover](/posts/batch-04/retatrutide-return-policy-uk/).

## Red flags

- No COA, or one that is not batch-specific
- No UK company details anywhere on the site
- Pricing far below every comparable listing
- Reviews that look templated or arrived in a short burst
- No mention of cold-chain packaging for a product that needs refrigeration

## Comparing sellers

Shortlist a few UK sellers and score each on COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details. The [guide to comparing retatrutide suppliers in the UK](/posts/batch-04/how-to-compare-retatrutide-suppliers-uk/) walks through this comparison step by step.

## First order tips

If you are new to a particular seller, consider starting with a smaller vial size to judge delivery speed, packaging and COA accuracy before committing to a larger order. Keep the outer packaging and delivery confirmation until you have checked the batch number against the COA.

## Bottom line

A UK retatrutide purchase comes down to matching a fair price per mg with a seller who is transparent about batch testing, company identity and delivery terms. Compare more than one listing before ordering, and treat a documented listing like Enzo Meds as a benchmark for what good practice looks like.
