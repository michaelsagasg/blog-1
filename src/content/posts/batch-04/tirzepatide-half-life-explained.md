Tirzepatide has a half-life of approximately five days, meaning roughly five days pass before the amount of drug in the bloodstream falls by half. That figure is why tirzepatide, whether as the licensed medicine Mounjaro or a research-grade peptide, is dosed once a week.

## What half-life means in practice

Half-life describes how quickly a drug clears from the body. Starting at a given level, after one half-life you are at 50% of that level, after two half-lives 25%, after three 12.5%. It is a standard way pharmacologists describe how long a drug's effects are likely to last and how often it needs to be taken to maintain steady levels.

## Why five days supports a weekly schedule

With a roughly five-day half-life, meaningful amounts of tirzepatide remain in the body for several weeks. A new weekly dose arrives before the previous one has fully cleared, which keeps levels comparatively steady between doses rather than causing sharp peaks and troughs.

## Why the half-life is this long

Naturally occurring GLP-1 and GIP hormones last only minutes in the body before being broken down by enzymes. Tirzepatide is engineered with a fatty acid side chain that allows it to bind to albumin, a blood protein, which protects it from rapid breakdown and slows its clearance by the kidneys. This is what extends its half-life from minutes to days.

## Reaching steady levels

Because each dose builds on what remains from the last, levels rise gradually over the first several weeks of consistent weekly dosing before reaching a steady state, typically after around four to five half-lives, roughly three to four weeks for tirzepatide.

## What happens after stopping

Levels fall by half roughly every five days after the final dose, following the same pattern in reverse, meaning it takes around three to four weeks to clear to a small fraction of peak levels. This describes general pharmacokinetics, not personal guidance on stopping any specific regimen, which should involve a clinician.

## What tirzepatide is

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. As Mounjaro, it holds a UK medicines licence for weight management and type 2 diabetes in eligible adults. It is also sold as a research-grade peptide by specialist sellers. Its half-life comes from the clinical pharmacology studies conducted during its trial programme, the same data that supported its licensing.

## Bottom line

A roughly five-day half-life is why tirzepatide is dosed weekly, whether as a licensed pen or a research peptide. It takes around three to four weeks of consistent dosing to reach steady levels, and a similar period after the last dose for levels to fall substantially.
