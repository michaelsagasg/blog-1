Retatrutide in the UK typically costs between £3.50 and £6 per mg, depending on the seller, the vial size and current demand. There is no fixed or regulated price, since it is sold as a research-grade product outside the licensed medicines system, so prices move seller to seller and change over time.

## Why there is no single price

Because retatrutide is not a licensed medicine, there is no NHS price list or standard pharmacy markup to anchor costs against. Each seller sets its own pricing based on sourcing, testing costs and demand. That makes comparison shopping more important than it would be for a licensed drug.

## Why larger vials usually cost less per mg

Packaging, shipping and lab testing carry a fixed cost regardless of vial size, so spreading that cost across more milligrams generally lowers the per-mg price on larger vials. This is not universal, since some sellers price flat across sizes, which is exactly why calculating price per mg matters more than comparing sticker prices.

## What affects the price beyond vial size

- **Testing and COA quality.** Sellers who commission third-party lab testing for every batch often price slightly higher than those relying on in-house or less frequent testing.
- **Brand and sourcing.** Some sellers source through named manufacturing partners and reflect that in price.
- **Delivery service level.** Next-day tracked delivery and cold-chain packaging add cost compared to standard shipping.
- **Demand.** Prices can shift with overall market demand for retatrutide at a given time.

## What the price does not tell you

A higher price does not automatically mean a better product, and a lower price does not automatically mean a worse one. What matters more than the number itself is whether the price is backed by a batch-specific COA, a transparent UK seller and clear delivery terms. ## Ongoing cost considerations

Beyond the price per vial, buyers sometimes factor in delivery charges, since some sellers offer free delivery above a spending threshold while others charge a flat fee regardless of order size. Check this at checkout rather than assuming it is included in the listed price.

## Bottom line

Expect to pay roughly £3.50 to £6 per mg for retatrutide from a UK seller, with larger vials usually cheaper per mg than smaller ones. Always calculate price per mg before comparing listings, and weigh price alongside COA transparency and seller details rather than choosing on cost alone.
