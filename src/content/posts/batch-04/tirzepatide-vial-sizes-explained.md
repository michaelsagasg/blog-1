Tirzepatide peptide vials in the UK typically range from around 5 mg up to 60 mg, with 10 mg, 30 mg and 60 mg being the most commonly listed sizes. This is separate from the licensed Mounjaro pen, which comes as a fixed-dose pre-filled device. A peptide vial is the total supply in the container, reconstituted before use.

## What vial size actually tells you

A 30 mg vial holds 30 mg of tirzepatide in total, usually as a freeze-dried powder mixed with a diluent like bacteriostatic water before use. The vial size reflects total supply, not a single dose, which depends on how much diluent is added and how much is drawn per use.

## Why bigger vials usually price lower per mg

Fixed costs, packaging, lab testing and delivery, get spread across more mg on a larger vial, which tends to lower the price per mg. This is a general pattern rather than a guarantee, so it is worth checking the actual numbers on each listing rather than assuming it.

## How buyers typically pick a size

- **First order with a new seller.** A smaller vial limits the cost of testing whether a seller delivers as promised.
- **An established, trusted seller.** A larger vial can work out cheaper per mg over time.
- **Stability limits.** Reconstituted peptide vials generally have a limited window once mixed, often cited as around 28 days refrigerated, so a large vial only pays off if it will genuinely be used within that time.

## The COA matters at every size

Whatever size you choose, look for a batch-specific certificate of analysis with a batch number matching the vial label. Enzo Meds is one example of a UK seller publishing a COA per batch across its tirzepatide size range, a useful reference point for what a complete listing looks like regardless of vial size.

## Calculating price per mg

Divide the listed price by the vial size in mg. A 10 mg vial at £44 is £4.40 per mg. A 45 mg vial at £175 is about £3.89 per mg. This is the number that actually tells you whether a bigger vial is a better deal from a given seller, not the total price.

## What tirzepatide does

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As Mounjaro, it is a licensed UK medicine in a fixed-dose pen. As a research-grade peptide sold by weight in a vial, pricing and sizing are set by individual sellers rather than a standard pharmacy pack.

## Bottom line

UK tirzepatide peptide vials commonly range from 5 mg to 60 mg, with larger sizes typically priced lower per mg. Pick a size that matches how much you expect to use within a reasonable storage window, and check the COA and batch number regardless of the size ordered.
