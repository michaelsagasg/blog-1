Before buying tirzepatide online from a UK peptide seller, check five things: a batch-specific certificate of analysis, the seller's UK company details, the price per mg, the delivery and storage terms, and the returns policy. This applies to the research-grade peptide market; if you would rather go through a licensed pharmacy, Mounjaro is available by private prescription and follows a different process.

## 1. Is there a batch-specific COA?

The certificate of analysis should be visible on the product page and show identity, purity and concentration testing for the specific batch you will receive. The batch number on the certificate needs to match the one printed on your vial. No COA, or a generic one that does not reference a batch number, is the clearest warning sign.

## 2. Who is behind the listing?

Look for a UK business name, an address or company number, and more than one way to contact them. A site offering nothing beyond a checkout page gives you little to fall back on if the order does not match what was promised.

## 3. What does it cost per mg?

Vial sizes vary between sellers, so divide the price by the mg content rather than comparing totals. A 30 mg vial at £140 is about £4.67 per mg. A 60 mg vial at £210 is £3.50 per mg. This figure is what actually lets you compare value across different listings.

## 4. How is it delivered and stored?

Tirzepatide vials generally need refrigeration, so check the listing mentions insulated or cold-pack packaging, along with a stated dispatch cutoff and delivery window. A seller offering next-day tracked delivery with cold-chain handling gives more assurance than one silent on the point.

## 5. What is the returns policy?

A written policy covering damaged, late or incorrect orders gives you something concrete to refer back to. If nothing is written down, you have little recourse if a problem comes up.

## Red flags to weigh heavily

- A COA not linked to a specific batch number
- Pricing well below every comparable UK listing
- No UK company details anywhere on the site
- Reviews that look templated or arrived in a short burst
- Payment limited to irreversible methods only

## What tirzepatide is

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As Mounjaro, it is a licensed UK medicine. As a research-grade peptide, it is sold without that oversight, which is exactly why these five checks matter more in that market than the price alone.

## Bottom line

Run every tirzepatide listing through the same five checks: COA, seller identity, price per mg, delivery and storage terms, and returns policy. A listing that is clear on all five is a safer choice than one that competes only on price.
