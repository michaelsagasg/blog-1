Buying tirzepatide for the first time in the UK means choosing between two routes: a private prescription for Mounjaro through a registered pharmacy, or a research-grade peptide vial from a specialist online seller. The steps and what to check differ between the two, so it helps to know which one you are actually looking at.

## The prescription route

Mounjaro is dispensed after an online or in-person assessment with a registered prescriber, who checks eligibility before a pharmacy dispenses a pre-filled pen. This route comes with the oversight of a licensed medicine, and cost typically includes the consultation as well as the product.

## The peptide-seller route

Outside the prescription pathway, tirzepatide is sold as a research-grade peptide in multi-dose vials by specialist UK sellers. This is a different market with different checks, focused on the seller's own transparency rather than a prescriber's assessment.

## What to check before a first peptide-seller order

- A certificate of analysis (COA) linked to the specific batch you will receive
- The vial size in mg, so you can calculate price per mg
- A real UK business name and more than one contact method
- Written delivery and returns terms

Our guide to [tirzepatide vial sizes explained](/posts/batch-04/tirzepatide-vial-sizes-explained/) covers the common size options if you are trying to decide what to order first.

## Placing the order

Peptide sellers typically run a standard online checkout, accepting card payment, bank transfer, and sometimes cryptocurrency. Card payment through a processor tends to give more recourse if the order does not arrive or does not match the listing.

## What happens after payment

A well-run seller confirms the order immediately and sends tracking once it dispatches. Orders placed before an early afternoon cutoff are often shipped the same day, with next-day delivery typical for UK addresses.

## What arrives

Expect discreet outer packaging with no indication of contents from the outside. Tirzepatide vials generally need refrigeration, so look for insulated packaging or a cold pack, particularly for orders placed in warmer months.

## Checking the batch number

Match the batch number on the vial label against the COA shown on the listing before you ordered. If they do not line up, or there is no batch number printed at all, contact the seller before using the product.

## Storage after delivery

Refrigerate the vial promptly unless the listing states otherwise, keep it away from direct light and heat, and label it with the date it arrived so you can track storage time.

## What tirzepatide does

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, participants on the highest studied dose lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As Mounjaro, it is licensed in the UK for weight management and type 2 diabetes in eligible adults following a prescriber's assessment. As a research-grade peptide, it is sold without that same oversight, which is why the seller's own COA and transparency matter more in that market.

## Common first-order questions

**Which route is right for me?** That depends on whether you want a licensed, prescriber-assessed pen or are comparing research-grade peptide listings; the two are not directly interchangeable.

**How long does peptide delivery take?** Usually one to three working days for a UK address, depending on the seller's dispatch process.

**What if the vial does not match the COA?** Contact the seller before using the product and ask them to explain the discrepancy.

## Bottom line

A first tirzepatide purchase starts with deciding between the licensed prescription route and the research-grade peptide market. If you go the peptide route, the checklist is the same as for any specialist seller: COA, seller identity, price per mg, delivery terms and returns policy, checked before you pay rather than after.
