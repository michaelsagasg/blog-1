Tirzepatide costs differ depending on which route you use. Research-grade peptide vials from UK sellers typically run £3.50 to £5 per mg. Mounjaro, the licensed prescription pen, is priced per pen through a private prescribing service, usually bundling the medicine with the prescriber's assessment, so it is not directly comparable to a per-mg peptide price.

## Two different markets, two different price structures

The licensed route prices the whole service, consultation included, per pen or per month of supply. The peptide-seller route prices a vial by its mg content, with no consultation involved, so the buyer takes on more responsibility for checking what they are getting.

## Why larger vials often cost less per mg

Testing, packaging and delivery costs are largely fixed regardless of vial size, so a larger vial tends to spread those costs over more mg, lowering the per-mg price. Not every seller prices this way, which is why checking each listing's own numbers matters more than assuming a pattern.

## What changes the price

- **Batch testing.** Sellers publishing third-party COAs for every batch sometimes price slightly higher than those without.
- **Delivery service.** Next-day tracked and cold-chain packaging cost more than standard shipping.
- **Sourcing.** Named manufacturing partners can affect price positioning.
- **Overall demand** for tirzepatide at a given time.

## Comparing sellers properly

Always calculate price per mg rather than comparing sticker prices. A 30 mg vial at £145 is about £4.83 per mg. A 60 mg vial at £210 is about £3.50 per mg, a notably better rate. ## Why the lowest price needs scrutiny

A listing priced far below comparable sellers is more likely a sign of a shortcut, in testing, storage or genuine sourcing, than an honest bargain. Weigh price against COA transparency and company details rather than choosing purely on cost.

## What price alone does not tell you

A higher price is not a guarantee of quality, and a lower one is not automatically a red flag on its own. What matters is whether the price is backed by a batch-specific COA and a transparent, contactable UK seller. Enzo Meds is one example of a listing that pairs its pricing with a visible COA per batch, a useful reference for what good documentation looks like.

## What tirzepatide does

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As Mounjaro, it holds a UK licence for weight management and type 2 diabetes in eligible adults after assessment. As a research-grade peptide, it carries none of that same regulatory oversight, which is why seller transparency matters more in that market.

## Other costs to factor in

Delivery fees vary. Some sellers offer free UK delivery above a spending threshold, while others charge a flat rate regardless of order size. Check this at checkout, since it can shift the real cost of a smaller order.

## Bottom line

Expect roughly £3.50 to £5 per mg for tirzepatide peptide vials in the UK, with the licensed Mounjaro prescription route priced separately as a per-pen service. Calculate price per mg before comparing sellers, and weigh cost against COA transparency rather than choosing on price alone.
