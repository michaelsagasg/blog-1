If you have never bought a peptide before, the terminology and the checklist can feel unfamiliar. This guide covers the basics: what a certificate of analysis is, how vial sizes and pricing work, what delivery looks like, and the warning signs to watch for on a first order.

## Start with the certificate of analysis

A certificate of analysis, or COA, is a lab report for a specific batch of product. It usually shows identity testing (confirming the vial contains what it claims to), purity, and concentration. A trustworthy seller publishes the COA on the product page and prints a matching batch number on the vial label. If you cannot find one, or it is not tied to a specific batch, treat that as a reason to keep looking rather than a minor gap.

## Understanding vial sizes and concentration

Peptides are sold as a total amount in a vial, measured in milligrams (mg), not as a per-dose product. A 10 mg vial contains 10 mg of peptide total, reconstituted with a diluent like bacteriostatic water to reach a usable concentration. The vial size you choose affects both total cost and how long a vial lasts, but this guide covers buying only, not dosing.

## Working out price per mg

To compare listings fairly, divide the price by the vial size. A 10 mg vial at £45 is £4.50 per mg. A 20 mg vial at £75 is £3.75 per mg. Larger vials are usually, but not always, cheaper per mg. Always run this calculation before judging whether a price is good value.

## What a trustworthy UK seller looks like

- A COA on the product page, linked to the batch you'll receive
- A UK business name, address or company number
- More than one way to contact them
- Clear delivery timeframes and a written returns policy
- A price per mg that is in line with, not wildly below, comparable sellers

Enzo Meds is one example of a UK listing built this way, with a COA shown per batch, useful as a reference point for what a complete listing looks like.

## What delivery usually looks like

Expect tracked shipping via Royal Mail or a courier, with next-day delivery common for UK orders placed before an early afternoon cutoff. Packaging is normally discreet on the outside, and insulated or cold-packed inside if the product needs refrigeration.

## Payment methods

Card payment through a processor is the most common option and gives you a dispute route through your bank if something goes wrong. Bank transfer and cryptocurrency are also offered by many sellers but are harder to reverse if an order fails to arrive.

## Red flags for a first-time buyer

- No COA anywhere on the site
- Reviews that all read similarly or appeared within a short window
- No UK company information
- Prices well below every other seller for the same product
- Payment options limited only to irreversible methods

## Checking your order on arrival

When the parcel arrives, check the outer packaging is undamaged and, if refrigeration is expected, that the contents still feel cool. Match the batch number on the vial to the COA you were shown before ordering. Refrigerate promptly and label the vial with the date it arrived.

## A note on what peptides are

Peptides used in this space, including retatrutide and tirzepatide, are short chains of amino acids that act on hormone receptors involved in appetite and metabolism. Some, like tirzepatide as Mounjaro, are licensed medicines with a defined regulatory pathway. Others, like retatrutide, remain investigational and are sold by specialist sellers as research-grade products, without the oversight that comes with a pharmacy dispensing a licensed medicine.
