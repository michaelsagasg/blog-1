Tirzepatide has two routes into the UK market. It is a licensed medicine, sold under the brand Mounjaro through registered pharmacies with a private prescription, and it is also sold unbranded by specialist online peptide sellers as a research-grade product. The two are not interchangeable, and knowing the difference matters before you compare prices.

## The prescription route

Mounjaro is prescribed in the UK by GPs and registered online prescribers for weight management or type 2 diabetes, following an assessment. It comes as a pre-filled pen with a fixed dose per click, dispensed by a pharmacy, with the product's regulatory status and manufacturing standards backed by the licence itself. Cost, appointment requirements and eligibility criteria vary by provider.

## The peptide-seller route

Outside the prescription pathway, tirzepatide is also listed by online sellers as a peptide product, usually in a multi-dose vial rather than a pre-filled pen. These listings sit outside the pharmacy system, so the checks that matter shift from "is this a registered pharmacy" to "does this seller show its batch testing."

## What to check on a peptide-seller listing

- **A batch-specific COA.** The batch number on the certificate should match the one printed on the vial.
- **Vial size in mg**, not just a price, so you can work out price per mg.
- **A named UK business**, not just a storefront with no company details.
- **Clear delivery and returns terms**, written on the site rather than answered only if you ask.

Enzo Meds is one example of a listing that publishes a COA per batch alongside its tirzepatide vials, and it is a useful reference for what a transparent listing looks like.

## Price per mg

Tirzepatide vials in the UK peptide market commonly run from around £2.50 to £5 per mg depending on vial size and brand, with larger vials usually cheaper per mg than small ones. Mounjaro through a private prescription is priced per pen rather than per mg, and includes the prescribing and dispensing service in the cost, so the two are not directly comparable on price alone.

## Delivery and packaging

Sellers on either route in the UK typically use Royal Mail Tracked or a courier, with next-day delivery standard for orders placed before an early afternoon cutoff. Peptide vials need cold-chain handling, so check whether the seller uses insulated packaging and states how the product was kept cool in transit. A listing that says nothing about temperature control for a product that needs refrigeration is worth a direct question before you order.

## Red flags on peptide-seller listings

- No COA, or a COA that is not tied to a specific batch number
- Pricing far below every comparable UK listing
- No UK company name or address anywhere on the site
- Reviews that look copy-pasted or arrived all at once

## What tirzepatide does

Tirzepatide activates both the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT trials, participants on the highest studied dose lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As Mounjaro, it is licensed in the UK for weight management and type 2 diabetes in eligible adults, following a prescriber's assessment. As a research-grade peptide sold outside that pathway, it carries none of the oversight that comes with a pharmacy dispensing a licensed pen, which is why COA transparency from the seller matters so much.

## Payment methods

Prescription services generally take card payment as part of a wider consultation fee. Peptide sellers usually offer card payment through a processor and often bank transfer, with some accepting cryptocurrency. Card payment gives more dispute recourse if an order does not arrive or does not match the listing.
