Price per mg is the only fair way to compare retatrutide listings, since vial sizes differ from seller to seller. UK listings typically work out to somewhere between £3.50 and £6 per mg once you do the calculation, though the exact figure depends on vial size, seller and current demand.

## The calculation

Price per mg = listed price divided by vial size in mg.

A 10 mg vial priced at £45 works out to £4.50 per mg. A 15 mg vial priced at £58 works out to about £3.87 per mg. This single number lets you compare listings that otherwise look nothing alike on the surface.

## Why the sticker price is misleading on its own

A 20 mg vial at £75 looks far more expensive than a 5 mg vial at £24 if you only look at the total. But the 20 mg vial works out to £3.75 per mg, while the 5 mg vial works out to £4.80 per mg. The smaller vial is actually the worse deal per mg, despite the lower sticker price.

## Why larger vials usually price lower per mg

Fixed costs, like packaging, lab testing and delivery, do not scale directly with vial size. Spreading them over more mg tends to lower the per-mg cost on larger vials, though not every seller follows this pattern, which is why the calculation matters more than the assumption.

## When a low price per mg is a warning sign

A price per mg noticeably below the range other UK sellers are charging is worth investigating rather than celebrating. It can mean a seller skipped batch testing, is using cheaper unverified sourcing, or simply will not deliver at all. Compare the price against the rest of the listing, COA presence, company details, delivery terms, before deciding a low price is a genuine deal.

## What you are paying for

Retatrutide remains an investigational drug, in Phase 3 trials and not approved for use anywhere. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. The price you pay covers a research-grade product, not a licensed medicine, and no price point changes that regulatory status.

## Tracking price changes over time

Because there is no fixed market price, it is worth checking a seller's pricing periodically rather than assuming a quote from months ago still holds. Demand, sourcing costs and competition among UK sellers all move the number over time.

## Bottom line

Divide price by vial size in mg every time you compare retatrutide listings. Expect a range of roughly £3.50 to £6 per mg across UK sellers, treat outliers on the low end with more scrutiny, not less, and pair the price per mg with COA and company checks before ordering.
