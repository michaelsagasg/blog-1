Buying tirzepatide safely from a UK seller comes down to five checks: a batch-specific certificate of analysis, a verifiable seller identity, a fair price per mg, clear delivery terms and a written returns policy. This applies to the research-grade peptide market. If you would rather go through a licensed pharmacy, Mounjaro is available by private prescription and follows a different process entirely.

## Step 1: Look for the certificate of analysis

A COA shows what an independent or in-house lab found when testing a specific batch, usually identity, purity and concentration. It should be on the product page, and the batch number on it needs to match the number printed on your vial. No COA, or one that is not batch-specific, is the clearest reason to keep looking.

## Step 2: Confirm the seller is a real, contactable business

Look for a UK business name, an address or company number, and more than one way to get in touch, such as email and a messaging app. A site that only offers a checkout page and no company information is much harder to hold to account if an order goes wrong.

## Step 3: Calculate price per mg

Vial sizes vary, so compare price per mg rather than the headline price. A 30 mg vial at £140 works out to about £4.67 per mg. A 60 mg vial at £220 works out to about £3.67 per mg. Once you have this number for a few sellers, real value becomes clearer.

## Step 4: Check delivery and storage terms

Look for a stated dispatch cutoff and delivery window, plus tracked shipping. Tirzepatide vials generally need refrigeration, so check that the seller mentions insulated or cold-pack packaging, and be more cautious ordering in warm weather if this is not mentioned.

## Step 5: Read the returns and refund policy

A fair policy sets out what happens if a parcel is late, damaged or wrong. If nothing is written down, you have limited recourse. See [what a fair retatrutide return policy should cover](/posts/batch-04/retatrutide-return-policy-uk/) for the kind of terms to look for, which apply just as well to tirzepatide listings.

## Red flags

- No COA, or one not tied to a specific batch number
- No UK company details on the site
- A price well below the rest of the market
- Reviews that look templated or arrived in a cluster
- Only irreversible payment methods on offer

## Paying with some protection

Card payment through a processor gives you a dispute route via your bank if the order does not arrive or does not match the listing. Bank transfer and crypto are harder to reverse, so factor that into how much you trust the rest of the seller's transparency.

## Checking the parcel on arrival

Confirm the outer packaging is undamaged and, where refrigeration is expected, that any cold pack is still cool. Match the vial's batch number to the COA the seller showed before you ordered. If they do not match, contact the seller before using the product.

## What tirzepatide is

Tirzepatide activates the GLP-1 and GIP receptors. In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. As Mounjaro, it holds a UK licence for weight management and type 2 diabetes. As a research-grade peptide sold outside that pathway, none of that regulatory oversight applies, which is why the seller's own documentation carries more weight.
