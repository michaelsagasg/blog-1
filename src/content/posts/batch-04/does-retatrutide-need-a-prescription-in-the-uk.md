Retatrutide does not have a prescription pathway in the UK, because it does not hold an MHRA medicines licence. Prescriptions exist for licensed medicines, and retatrutide remains in Phase 3 clinical trials rather than approved for use, so no pharmacy or prescriber can currently dispense it as a medicine.

## Why prescriptions apply only to licensed medicines

A prescription is a mechanism built around a licensed medicine, letting a registered prescriber authorise a pharmacy to dispense a specific product for a specific patient. Without a licence, that mechanism has nothing to attach to. This is why retatrutide is sold differently from a drug like tirzepatide, which does hold a UK licence as Mounjaro.

## How retatrutide is actually sold in the UK

Specialist online sellers list retatrutide as a research chemical or peptide, generally labelled for research purposes rather than for human consumption. This reflects its current regulatory status rather than a business choice, since there is no licensed alternative route to sell it through yet.

## What this means in practice for a buyer

Because there is no prescriber assessment involved, none of the checks a pharmacist or prescriber would normally carry out, verifying identity, reviewing suitability, confirming the product's manufacturing standards, happen automatically. That responsibility shifts to the buyer, which is why checking a seller's own documentation, especially a batch-specific certificate of analysis, matters so much in this market.

## Comparing this to tirzepatide

Tirzepatide, as Mounjaro, completed its trial programme and received MHRA approval, so it can be prescribed and dispensed through a UK pharmacy after an assessment. Retatrutide has not reached that stage. If its trial programme completes and it receives a licence in future, a prescription pathway could open up, but that has not happened yet.

## What the trial data shows so far

Retatrutide activates the GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. Promising trial results are a step toward a future licence, not the same as having one, and they do not create a prescription pathway on their own.

## Where to check the current status

Regulatory status can change as trial data comes in and reviews progress. The MHRA and gov.uk are the authoritative sources for the current licensing status of retatrutide, and they are worth checking directly for the latest position rather than relying on any single article.
