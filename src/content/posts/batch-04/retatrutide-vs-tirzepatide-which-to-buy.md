Retatrutide and tirzepatide differ in three practical ways that matter before you buy: what receptors they act on, what their trial data shows, and their UK regulatory status. Tirzepatide is a licensed UK medicine available by prescription as Mounjaro, and also sold as a research peptide. Retatrutide is investigational only, sold exclusively as a research peptide, with no licensed route in the UK yet.

## What each drug targets

Tirzepatide activates two receptors: GLP-1 and GIP. Retatrutide activates three: GLP-1, GIP and glucagon. That extra glucagon receptor activity is the main mechanistic difference between the two, and it is part of why retatrutide's trial results have drawn significant attention.

## What the trial data shows

In Eli Lilly's SURMOUNT-1 trial, the highest dose group taking tirzepatide lost an average of around 21% of body weight over 72 weeks. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial for retatrutide, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks, a shorter trial period. Both are company-reported averages from specific trial populations, not a guarantee of any individual's result, and the different trial lengths make a direct comparison imprecise.

## Regulatory status in the UK

Tirzepatide holds an MHRA licence as Mounjaro, so it can be prescribed and dispensed through a UK pharmacy. Retatrutide remains in Phase 3 trials with no UK licence, so it is sold only through specialist sellers as a research product. This difference matters for how much oversight sits behind each purchase.

## Price comparison

Tirzepatide peptide vials in the UK typically run £3.30 to £5 per mg. Retatrutide typically runs £3.50 to £6 per mg. Retatrutide's newer market position and different manufacturing scale contribute to its generally slightly higher price per mg, though this varies by seller and vial size.

## Which one fits your situation

- If you want a licensed medicine with prescriber oversight, tirzepatide as Mounjaro is the only one of the two with that option currently.
- If you are comparing research-grade peptides specifically, both are sold through the same type of specialist seller market, and the choice comes down to which compound's trial profile and receptor activity interests you.
- Neither purchase decision should be based on price alone; COA transparency and seller trustworthiness matter regardless of which compound you choose.

## What neither purchase guarantees

Trial averages describe study populations over defined periods, not individual outcomes. Neither compound's trial results translate into a promise about what any specific person will experience, and no UK seller of either product, licensed or unlicensed, can responsibly claim otherwise.

## Bottom line

Tirzepatide offers a licensed UK route through Mounjaro alongside a research-peptide market. Retatrutide is available only through the research-peptide market for now, with generally higher reported trial weight loss over a shorter study period. Whichever you are weighing, run the same seller checklist, COA, company details, delivery terms, before deciding where to buy.
