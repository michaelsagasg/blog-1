There is no single "best" place to buy retatrutide online in the UK, because it depends on what you weigh most: price, delivery speed, or how much proof a seller gives you about what is actually in the vial. What you can do is score any UK seller against the same four criteria and see where they land.

## The four things that separate a good listing from a weak one

1. **COA transparency.** Is there a certificate of analysis for the specific batch you would receive, with a batch number that matches the vial label?
2. **Price per mg.** Not the headline price, but the price divided by the vial size in mg, so you can compare across different pack sizes.
3. **Delivery terms.** Stated dispatch cutoff, delivery method and expected arrival time, plus whether cold-chain packaging is mentioned.
4. **Company transparency.** A real UK business name and contact details, not just a checkout page.

## Scoring a listing

Open the product page and look for each of the four points directly on the page, not buried in a policy document elsewhere on the site. A strong listing answers all four without you having to ask. Enzo Meds is one example that publishes a batch-specific COA alongside its retatrutide listings, which is a useful reference for what "good" looks like on this point.

## Price per mg as your comparison tool

Retatrutide in the UK is typically sold in vials or pens from around 2 mg up to 20 mg or more. A 10 mg vial priced at £45 works out to £4.50 per mg. A 20 mg vial at £75 works out to £3.75 per mg, a better rate despite the higher headline price. Always do this calculation before deciding a seller is "cheap" or "expensive."

## Why the cheapest listing is not automatically the best

A price that sits well below every other UK seller is more often a sign of a shortcut somewhere, whether that is skipped lab testing, poor storage, or a listing that will not deliver at all, rather than a genuine bargain. Our article on [whether the cheapest retatrutide listing is ever the right choice](/posts/batch-04/is-the-cheapest-retatrutide-listing-ever-the-right-choice/) covers this trade-off in more depth.

## Delivery and packaging to expect

Look for Royal Mail Tracked or a courier with next-day UK delivery as standard, alongside insulated or cold-pack packaging given that retatrutide typically needs refrigeration. If a seller does not mention how the product is kept cool in transit, ask before ordering, particularly in summer.

## Reading reviews without being misled

Reviews can help, but a wall of near-identical five-star reviews posted within days of each other is a weaker signal than a smaller number of detailed reviews that mention specifics like delivery time or COA accuracy. Our guide on [how to read retatrutide customer reviews without being misled](/posts/batch-04/how-to-read-retatrutide-customer-reviews/) has a fuller checklist.

## Red flags that should move a seller down your list

- No COA, or one not linked to a specific batch
- No UK company name or address
- Reviews that read like templates
- Delivery terms that are vague or missing entirely
- A price that undercuts the rest of the market by a wide margin

## Putting it together

Rather than searching for a single "best" answer, shortlist two or three UK sellers and score each on COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details. The one that is strongest across all four, not just cheapest, is the better choice for most buyers. This is also the approach used in our broader [how to compare retatrutide suppliers in the UK](/posts/batch-04/how-to-compare-retatrutide-suppliers-uk/) guide.

## What you are actually buying

Retatrutide remains an investigational drug, still in Phase 3 trials and not approved for use anywhere. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 study, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. No UK seller offering it as a research product can make outcome guarantees, and any listing that does is overstating what is known.

## Bottom line

The best place to buy retatrutide online in the UK is whichever seller scores highest on COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details, not whichever comes up first in a search or has the lowest headline price. Score a few listings side by side before you decide.
