Retatrutide is sold in the UK in vials ranging from around 2 mg up to 20 mg or more, with 5 mg, 10 mg and 15 mg being the most commonly listed sizes. The vial size is the total amount of peptide in the container, not a single dose, and larger vials generally, though not always, cost less per mg than smaller ones.

## What "vial size" actually means

A 10 mg vial contains 10 mg of retatrutide in total, typically as a freeze-dried powder that gets reconstituted with a diluent like bacteriostatic water before use. The vial size tells you the total supply, not the strength of an individual dose, which depends on how much diluent is added and how much is drawn per use.

## Why larger vials usually cost less per mg

Packaging, lab testing and delivery carry fixed costs regardless of vial size, so a larger vial spreads those costs across more mg. That tends to lower the price per mg on bigger sizes, though it is not a universal rule, so it is worth checking each listing's own numbers rather than assuming the pattern holds.

## How buyers typically choose a size

- **Trying a new seller.** A smaller vial limits spend while you assess delivery speed, packaging and COA accuracy.
- **An established seller you already trust.** A larger vial often works out cheaper per mg for an ongoing relationship.
- **Storage limits.** Reconstituted peptides generally have a limited stability window once mixed, commonly cited as around 28 days refrigerated for bacteriostatic-water preparations, so a very large vial is only cost-effective if it will actually be used within that window.

## Checking the COA regardless of size

Every vial size should come with its own batch-specific certificate of analysis, with a batch number matching the vial label. Vial size does not change how important this check is. Enzo Meds is one example of a UK seller that lists a COA per batch across its size range, useful as a reference for what a complete listing should include regardless of the size chosen.

## Working out price per mg yourself

Divide the listed price by the mg in the vial. A 5 mg vial at £25 is £5 per mg. A 15 mg vial at £55 is about £3.67 per mg. Comparing this figure, not the total price, is what tells you whether a larger vial is actually a better deal for a given seller.

## A note on what retatrutide is

Retatrutide activates the GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. It remains an investigational compound without a UK medicines licence, so vial size and pricing are set by individual sellers rather than a regulated price list.

## Bottom line

UK retatrutide vials typically range from 2 mg to 20 mg, with larger sizes usually, but not always, priced lower per mg. Choose a size based on how much you expect to use within a reasonable storage window, and check that the COA and batch number match regardless of which size you pick.
