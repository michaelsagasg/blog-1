Retatrutide is not sold in UK pharmacies or through the NHS. It is an investigational drug, still in Phase 3 trials, so the only current route to buy it is through online sellers who list it as a research or peptide product. There is no single "official" UK shop, which is exactly why comparing listings matters.

## What a UK listing usually includes

Most retatrutide sellers in the UK operate as small specialist businesses rather than registered pharmacies. A listing worth trusting typically shows:

- The exact peptide name and vial or pen size in mg
- A batch number linked to a certificate of analysis (COA)
- A UK business address or company number in the footer or About page
- Stated dispatch and delivery timelines
- A price per mg, not just a headline price

Enzo Meds is one example of a UK listing built this way, with a COA attached to each batch on the product page. Use it, or any seller, as a reference point for what a complete listing looks like rather than treating any single name as the only option.

## Vial sizes and pricing patterns

Retatrutide is typically sold in single-use pens or multi-dose vials ranging from 2 mg to 20 mg, sometimes higher. Larger sizes usually cost more per mg to buy but less per mg overall, since fixed costs like the vial, packaging and testing are spread across more product. Expect UK prices to sit roughly in the £3 to £6 per mg range, though this shifts with vial size, brand and current demand.

## What to check before you order

1. **Is there a COA for this specific batch, not a generic one?** A batch number on the vial should match the batch number on the report.
2. **Does the listing say where the product is tested?** Third-party lab testing is worth more than an in-house claim.
3. **Is the price per mg competitive without being an outlier?** A price far below the market average is a warning sign, not a bargain.
4. **What does the returns and delivery policy say?** A seller with nothing written down has nothing to hold them to.

## Delivery expectations

UK sellers generally dispatch via Royal Mail or a tracked courier, with next-day delivery common for orders placed before an early afternoon cutoff. Packaging is usually discreet, meaning the outer box gives no indication of the contents, and insulated where cold-chain handling is claimed. If a seller does not mention temperature control for a peptide that needs refrigeration, ask before you order.

## Payment methods to expect

Most UK peptide sellers accept debit or credit cards through a payment processor, and many also take bank transfer. Some accept cryptocurrency. Card payments give you more recourse if something goes wrong, since the transaction runs through a bank's dispute process. If a seller only accepts direct bank transfer or crypto with no other option, weigh that against the rest of the listing rather than treating it as an automatic red flag or an automatic green light.

## Red flags worth knowing

- No company name, address or contact method beyond a contact form
- Stock photos instead of the actual product and batch
- Prices that undercut every other UK seller by a wide margin
- No mention of lab testing anywhere on the site
- Reviews that all read the same or appeared in a short burst

## Comparing more than one seller

Before ordering, it is worth opening two or three UK listings side by side and comparing price per mg, COA presence, delivery timeframe and stated company details. ## A note on what retatrutide is

Retatrutide activates three gut hormone receptors, GLP-1, GIP and glucagon. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, participants on the highest dose lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. It is not approved for use anywhere in the world yet, and sellers listing it are selling an investigational compound, not a licensed medicine. This article does not recommend a dose or a specific seller. It is a starting point for evaluating any UK listing on its own merits.

There is no pharmacy shelf for retatrutide in the UK. Buyers compare specialist online sellers on COA transparency, price per mg, delivery terms and company details.
