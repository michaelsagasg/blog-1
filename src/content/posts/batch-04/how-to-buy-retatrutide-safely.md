Buying retatrutide safely comes down to five checks before you pay: a batch-specific COA, a real seller identity, a fair price per mg, clear delivery terms and a written returns policy. Work through them in order and you will filter out most of the risk before you enter payment details.

## Step 1: Find the certificate of analysis

Look for a certificate of analysis (COA) on the product page itself, not buried in a separate document you have to request. The COA should show identity, purity and concentration testing results, and the batch number on it should match the batch number printed on the vial you receive. If a seller cannot show this before you order, that is reason enough to look elsewhere.

## Step 2: Check who you are actually buying from

A trustworthy UK seller shows a business name, a UK address or company number, and more than one way to contact them, such as email plus WhatsApp or a phone line. A site with only a checkout form and no company information behind it is harder to hold accountable if something goes wrong.

## Step 3: Work out the real price per mg

Retatrutide is sold in a range of vial and pen sizes, so compare price per mg rather than the sticker price. Divide the listed price by the milligrams in the vial. A 10 mg vial at £45 is £4.50 per mg. A 15 mg vial at £60 is £4 per mg. Once you have this figure for a few sellers, you can see which one is actually offering better value, not just a lower number on the page.

## Step 4: Read the delivery terms

Look for a stated dispatch cutoff, an estimated delivery window, and confirmation of tracked shipping. Because retatrutide typically needs refrigeration, check whether the seller mentions insulated packaging or a cold pack, particularly if you are ordering in warmer months.

## Step 5: Check the returns and refund policy

A fair policy explains what happens if a parcel arrives damaged, late or wrong. If there is no written policy at all, you have no real recourse if something goes wrong with the order. Our guide on [what a fair retatrutide return policy should cover](/posts/batch-04/retatrutide-return-policy-uk/) breaks this down further.

## Red flags that should stop you

- No COA, or a COA with no batch number
- A price dramatically below every comparable listing
- No UK company details anywhere on the site
- Reviews that all read the same or appeared in a short burst
- Payment only by irreversible methods with no card option

## Paying in a way that protects you

Card payment through a payment processor gives you a dispute route through your bank if the order does not arrive or does not match what was listed. Bank transfer and cryptocurrency payments are harder to reverse, so weigh that against the rest of the seller's transparency before choosing that route.

## When the parcel arrives

Check the outer packaging is intact and, if refrigeration is expected, that any cold pack still feels cool. Match the batch number on the vial label against the COA you were shown before ordering. If either does not line up, contact the seller before use.

## What retatrutide is, and is not

Retatrutide is an investigational drug that activates the GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors. In Eli Lilly's Phase 2 trial, the highest dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight at 48 weeks. It is not approved for use anywhere yet, so any UK seller is offering a research-grade product, not a licensed medicine, and no seller can guarantee a specific outcome from using it.
