At the time of writing, [Enzo Meds](https://enzomeds.com?r=blog) lists retatrutide at £155 for a 30 mg pen and £210 for a 60 mg pen. That works out to £5.17 per mg and £3.50 per mg. The 60 mg pen cuts the price per mg by about a third.

The rest of this page turns those prices into weekly and monthly numbers so you can plan a budget.

## The three formulas

Cost per mg = pen price divided by milligrams in the pen.

Cost per week = cost per mg multiplied by weekly milligrams.

Cost per month = cost per week multiplied by 4.3.
