## What a batch test tells you

A certificate of analysis (COA) is a lab report for one production batch. A typical report shows:

- The compound name and batch number
- Purity, often measured by HPLC (a method that separates compounds in a sample)
- Identity, often confirmed by mass spectrometry (a method that measures molecular weight)
- The lab name and test date

That gives you facts about what was in a sample from the batch you are buying from.
