## Weeks of supply by size

Weeks of supply = total milligrams divided by weekly milligrams.

| Weekly amount | 10 mg | 15 mg | 20 mg | 30 mg | 60 mg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 mg | 5 weeks | 7.5 weeks | 10 weeks | 15 weeks | 30 weeks |
| 4 mg | 2.5 weeks | 3.75 weeks | 5 weeks | 7.5 weeks | 15 weeks |
| 6 mg | 1.7 weeks | 2.5 weeks | 3.3 weeks | 5 weeks | 10 weeks |
| 8 mg | 1.25 weeks | 1.9 weeks | 2.5 weeks | 3.75 weeks | 7.5 weeks |

These are arithmetic examples, not dosing advice. Trials raised doses in steps, and [Retatrutide Dose Escalation in Trials](/posts/batch-03/retatrutide-dose-escalation-in-trials/) covers how.

## Match the size to your plan

1. Decide how many weeks you want covered.
2. Multiply by your weekly milligrams.
3. Pick the smallest pen that reaches that total, or order two.

Example: 12 weeks at 4 mg a week is 48 mg. The 60 mg pen covers it with 12 mg to spare. The 30 mg pen would need a second order.
