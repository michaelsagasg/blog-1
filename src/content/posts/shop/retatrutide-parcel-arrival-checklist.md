When your order arrives, unpack it the same day, match the batch number to the COA on the product page, and put it in the fridge. Ten minutes covers all of it.

[Enzo Meds](https://enzomeds.com?r=blog) dispatches the same day for orders placed before 2pm and delivers next day in the UK, so the parcel often arrives when you are ready for it. Here is what to do when it does.

## Step 1: Look at the outer packaging

Check the box or envelope before you open it. Note crushed corners, wet patches or torn tape. If anything looks off, photograph it. It costs nothing, and it helps if you need to message support.

## Step 2: Unpack on a clean surface

Wash your hands and use a clean, dry surface. Keep the packing slip, order number and any inserts in one place.

## Step 3: Inspect the product

Look over each item:

| Look at | What you want to see |
|---|---|
| Body of the pen or vial | No cracks or chips |
| Seals and caps | Intact and in place |
| Label | Readable, with a batch number |
| Contents, if visible | Even, dry powder with no discolouration |

Freeze-dried peptide can look like a white cake, a thin film or loose powder. Small amounts of powder on the glass are common after shipping.

## Step 4: Match the batch number to the COA

Enzo Meds says every batch is verified and that the certificate of analysis (COA) is on the product listing. Open the listing, find the COA and compare the batch number to the one on your product. When they match, the report describes what you are holding. Our guide to [reading a peptide certificate of analysis](/posts/batch-03/how-to-read-a-peptide-certificate-of-analysis/) explains the lines on the report.

## Step 5: Store it

Follow the storage guidance on the product listing first. As a general rule for peptides, keep them in the fridge at 2 to 8 degrees Celsius, away from light, upright in a small lidded box, and not in the door or near the freezer compartment. For the details, see [how to store reconstituted peptides](/posts/batch-03/how-to-store-reconstituted-peptides/).

## Step 6: Plan before you open anything

Know what you need before you begin: swabs, a sharps bin and a note of your plan. If your product needs mixing, work out the volume first with [how much bacteriostatic water to mix with peptides](/posts/batch-02/how-much-bacteriostatic-water-to-mix-with-peptides/) and avoid the mistakes in [common mistakes in peptide reconstitution](/posts/batch-02/common-mistakes-in-peptide-reconstitution/). Our [starter supplies list](/posts/shop/retatrutide-starter-supplies-shopping-list/) covers the rest.

## Step 7: Keep records

Save a short note with:

- Order date and delivery date
- Order number
- Batch number and the COA
- The date you first used the product

Take a photo of the label and of the COA page. It takes a minute and saves searching later.

## If something is wrong

Message Enzo Meds the same day. It offers 1:1 support on WhatsApp, and also Telegram and the website contact form, and says it replies within minutes during business hours. Send:

- Your order number
- Photos of the packaging and the item
- The batch number

Reporting on the day of delivery keeps the details fresh and makes a fix quicker.

## Common questions after delivery

### The powder looks different from a photo I saw. Is that a problem?

Freeze-dried peptide varies in appearance. A solid cake, a thin film or loose powder are all common. If you see yellow or brown colour or wet clumps, photograph it and message support.

### The parcel sat outside for a few hours. What now?

Bring it in and put it in the fridge. Note the time and weather in your records. If you are unsure, send the details to support and ask.

### Where do I find the COA again?

On the product listing at Enzo Meds. Save a copy of the page when you order so you have it later.

### How soon should I use it?

Follow the guidance on the product listing. Until you are ready, keep it in the fridge.
